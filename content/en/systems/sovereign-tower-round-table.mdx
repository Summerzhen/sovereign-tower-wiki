## Quick Answer

The Round Table is where you turn audience requests into knight assignments. Before sending a knight out, compare visible quest requirements, required stats, trait fit, armor state, loyalty, affinity, and whether that knight is needed for another deadline.

Use the [Quest Database](/quests) for quest filters and the [Knights Database](/knights) for stats, traits, meals, evolution, and best assignments.

## Assignment Checklist

| Check | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Required stats | The safest match starts with the visible quest requirement |
| Traits and hidden traits | Traits can improve, twist, or sabotage the expected result |
| Armor and health risk | A good match can still die if sent out damaged |
| Loyalty / affinity | A resentful knight may create refusal, departure, or unexpected branch risk |
| Availability | Long quests remove a knight from other urgent assignments |
| Route consequence | Some quests affect recruitment, endings, factions, or later dialogue |

## Round Table Workflow

1. Finish the morning audience and list every accepted problem.
2. Sort quests by deadline, danger, and route importance.
3. Assign must-succeed quests before optional errands.
4. Use equipment or potions to cover one weakness, not an entire mismatch.
5. Leave flexibility for returning knights, repairs, and follow-up audiences.
6. Record the outcome before rewinding or moving to the next cycle.

## Role Coverage Table

| Quest Need | Useful Knight Profile |
|---|---|
| Combat / hunt | Strength, Agility, armor, and low betrayal risk |
| Diplomacy | Charisma, stable loyalty, and social traits |
| Investigation | Wit, Luck, and traits connected to observation or secrets |
| Magic / strange event | Magic, supernatural tolerance, and low fear/rivalry risk |
| Public-order route | Charisma or Strength plus faction-compatible choices |

## Common Round Table Mistakes

| Mistake | Better Habit |
|---|---|
| Picking only the highest stat | Check disliked quest types and hidden trait risk |
| Sending damaged knights anyway | Repair armor or choose a lower-risk task |
| Filling every knight slot every cycle | Keep room for urgent follow-ups when possible |
| Ignoring minor failures | Minor failures can become route or ending evidence later |
| Rewinding without notes | Write down the failed assignment before changing the timeline |

## FAQ

### Is the Round Table only about stats?

No. Stats matter first, but traits, armor, affinity, loyalty, route state, and equipment can all change the final result.

### Should I always assign the best knight?

Not always. Save your strongest match for the quest with the highest deadline, death risk, or route consequence.

### Can Round Table choices affect endings?

Yes. Quest outcomes can change faction state, recruitment, romance, and late-route flags, so important assignments should be logged.
