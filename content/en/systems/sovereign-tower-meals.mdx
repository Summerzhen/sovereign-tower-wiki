## Why Meals Matter in Sovereign Tower

Feeding your knights the right food is one of the easiest ways to stabilize affinity and improve quest planning in Sovereign Tower. Community notes report that favorite meals can provide affinity gains and may also help with quest scoring, but exact values should be checked in the active save before you plan around them.

There are only six dishes in the entire game, and every knight has specific favorites. This guide breaks down how the meal system works, lists every knight's preferred food, and offers practical tips for managing your kitchen efficiently.

## How the Meal System Works

### The Six Dishes

The game offers exactly six meals you can prepare for your knights:

- **Galette-Saucisse**
- **Croque-Monsieur**
- **Préfou**
- **Crêpe**
- **Brizhian Butter Shortbread**
- **Lion's Taco**

Each knight responds to specific dishes from this list. Serving a meal they enjoy triggers two mechanical effects:

1. **Affinity support** �?A useful boost when a knight is close to a resignation or story threshold.
2. **Possible quest support** �?A small bonus reported by players, best confirmed in your active save.

The game may record that you've learned a knight's taste after feeding them a favorite. Check the UI behavior in your current save before assuming the preference is permanently logged.

### Why Affinity Is Critical

Affinity ranges from **-10 to +10**, and every knight begins at **0**. It increases when you assign quests they like, feed them favorite meals, or make sovereign decisions matching their preferred archetype. It decreases for disliked quests and conditions.

If affinity drops too low, that knight may queue a resignation audience and leave. Reported thresholds vary by character and build, so Check the current value before relying on a specific cutoff.

Affinity also gates **free-time conversations**, which are the primary way you unlock each knight's story content. High affinity means more story; low affinity means locked dialogue and a knight on the verge of quitting.

### The Knight Bonus Explained

Favorite foods are also discussed by players as part of quest optimization. If a food bonus appears in your active save, use it as one piece of a larger setup rather than as a substitute for the right knight, gear, and quest match.

In practical terms, meals are most useful when a knight is already close to the result you want. The safest approach is to combine favorite food, known traits, equipment, and a favorable quest type before sending them into a borderline mission.

## Strategic Meal Tips

### Prioritize High-Risk Knights First

Knights with the **Touchy** hidden attribute—like Victoria—suffer doubled affinity losses. Victoria already dislikes Crowd conditions and Diplomacy quests, and her hidden preference makes Diplomacy even worse. Feeding her favorite meals (Croque-Monsieur and Préfou) regularly is essential to counterbalance those losses before they spiral.

Similarly, knights who dislike multiple quest types or conditions are harder to keep happy through assignments alone. Meals become your most reliable affinity tool for them.

### Feed Before Dangerous Quests

If you're about to send a knight on a quest with conditions they dislike, pre-feed them a confirmed favorite meal. The affinity buffer can help offset a bad assignment, especially for knights who have not built up a loyalty cushion yet.

### Learn Tastes Early

Early in your playthrough, prioritize discovering each knight's tastes by serving them different dishes and watching how the game records the result. Once a preference is visible in your active save, you can plan meal rotations with more confidence.

### Don't Waste Meals on Chester and Dulahan

Since Chester and Dulahan enjoy all six meals, they'll gain the affinity and score bonus from literally anything you serve. Save your rarer dishes—especially Lion's Taco—for knights who specifically request them. Feed Chester and Dulahan whatever you have in surplus.

### Watch the Demission Threshold

If you notice a knight trending downward after several bad quest assignments, immediately feed them a confirmed favorite meal and follow with a favorable quest type or liked sovereign decision. Meals are best used before a loyalty problem becomes urgent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many meals are in Sovereign Tower?

There are exactly six meals: Galette-Saucisse, Croque-Monsieur, Préfou, Crêpe, Brizhian Butter Shortbread, and Lion's Taco. Every knight's preferences are drawn from this list. Two knights—Chester and Dulahan—enjoy all six, while most others have two favorites. Daguez and Goberto each have three.

### What does feeding a favorite Sovereign Tower meal actually do?

Serving a knight a dish they like is reported to grant affinity support and may provide a quest-related bonus. Use the in-game result screen or character notes to check exact values in the active save.

### Can I feed a knight a meal they don't like?

Based on community reports, serving a non-favorite meal appears to have no negative effect—it simply doesn't grant the affinity or score bonuses. You won't lose affinity for trying the wrong dish, so experimenting to discover preferences is safe. Once you find a favorite, the game remembers it.

### Which Sovereign Tower meal should I stock the most?

**Crêpe** is the best all-around choice, liked by 11 of the 22 knights with specific preferences. **Brizhian Butter Shortbread** comes in second with 9 fans. If you're building a general-purpose stockpile, prioritize these two. Save Lion's Taco—liked by only 4 knights—for when you specifically need to keep Brunhilda, Goberto, Ursula, or Zolta happy.
