## Strategies for Consistent Tower Clears

Earning the Sovereign Tower dragon heart is not a one-time luck event. Most players need multiple attempts before securing it. Developing a reliable strategy reduces frustration and makes each run more productive.

### Pre-Run Checklist

Before entering the tower, run through this checklist to make sure you are prepared:

- [ ] Class selected and leveled appropriately
- [ ] Wand upgraded to current maximum
- [ ] Traits assigned for tower-specific challenges
- [ ] Consumables stocked for boss encounters
- [ ] Previous run reviewed for mistakes

### During the Run

Once inside the tower, keep these principles in mind:

- **Pace Yourself**: Do not burn cooldowns on easy waves. Save resources for bosses and difficulty spikes.
- **Learn Boss Patterns**: The Dragon Knight boss has recognizable attack patterns. Dying to a boss once is learning; dying to the same attack twice is avoidable.
- **Adapt on the Fly**: If a wave feels harder than expected, adjust your positioning and ability usage rather than forcing the same approach.
- **Track Your Progress**: Note which waves give you trouble so you can adjust your build before the next attempt.

### Post-Run Review

After each attempt, take a moment to evaluate what went wrong and what went right. This reflection is where most improvement happens.

| Review Question | Why It Matters |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many players undermine their own Sovereign Tower dragon heart runs by falling into predictable traps. Recognizing these mistakes ahead of time can save you hours of wasted effort.

- **Ignoring Build Synergy**: Equipping strong individual pieces that do not work together will fail at higher waves.
- **Skipping Preparation**: Jumping into the tower without checking your traits, wand, or consumables is a recipe for early exits.
- **Overcommitting to Offense**: Damage means nothing if you cannot survive long enough to deal it.

- **Neglecting Boss Mechanics**: Every boss has tells. Learning them is more valuable than raw stat increases.
