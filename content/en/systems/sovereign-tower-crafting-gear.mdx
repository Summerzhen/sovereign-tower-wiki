## Quick Answer

Crafting and gear should support a good assignment, not rescue a terrible one. Start with the right knight, then use forge items, repairs, potions, or annex support to cover a narrow weakness before high-risk quests.

Use this page with the [Blacksmith Guide](/systems/sovereign-tower-blacksmith), [Annexes & Buildings](/systems/sovereign-tower-annexes-buildings), and [Quest Finder](/quests).

## Gear Planning Table

| Gear Goal | Use It When |
|---|---|
| Patch one missing stat | The knight is otherwise a strong fit for the quest |
| Protect armor | The quest has death or damage risk and the knight must survive |
| Push critical success | The reward or route consequence justifies spending resources |
| Enable investigation | Wit or Luck is close but not quite enough |
| Support strange routes | Magic, potion, or supernatural support may matter |

## Crafting Priority

| Route Problem | First Gear Question |
|---|---|
| Combat route | Is armor repaired, and does the knight meet Strength / Agility needs? |
| Diplomacy route | Is Charisma enough, and will the knight's personality create risk? |
| Investigation route | Can gear or potion support Wit / Luck without weakening survival? |
| Magic route | Is the route asking for Magic, Wit, or both? |
| Dragon Knight pressure | Which four knights must be alive, equipped, and available? |

## Do Not Overuse Gear

| Bad Habit | Safer Habit |
|---|---|
| Sending the wrong knight with expensive gear | Pick the right knight first, then improve the margin |
| Spending all resources on one quest | Keep money for repairs, audiences, and annex needs |
| Ignoring affinity and loyalty | A boosted knight can still produce a bad route result |
| Treating every item as confirmed | Record item effect, stat change, and quest result before publishing it |

## FAQ

### Should I craft before every quest?

No. Craft before high-risk, deadline, route-lock, or death-risk quests. Routine errands should not drain your resources.

### Can gear change quest outcomes?

It can, especially when the knight is close to the required stat threshold. Record the before-and-after result before treating the item as a confirmed solution.

### Is armor repair more important than stat gear?

For dangerous quests, often yes. A stat boost is useless if the knight dies or returns too damaged for the next required route.
