## Quick Answer

Build annexes around the problems your current route is actually producing. If knights are taking damage, prioritize forge and repair support. If magical or strange quests are appearing, track alchemy and supernatural support. If recruitment is blocked by capacity or staff access, document which building opened the slot.

Use this page with the [Map Guide](/systems/sovereign-tower-map), [Blacksmith Guide](/systems/sovereign-tower-blacksmith), and [Quest Database](/quests).

## Building Priority Table

| Annex / Building | Main Value | Track Before Upgrading |
|---|---|---|
| Intendancy | Rumors, knight secrets, route intelligence | Which knight clue it reveals and whether it affects assignment choices |
| Forge / Blacksmith | Gear, armor repair, physical quest support | Armor state, repair limit, item bonus, and failed quest avoided |
| Witch / Alchemy Room | Potions, magic support, strange-route preparation | Magic requirement, potion effect, cost, and route consequence |
| Map / Gallery | Region access, damaged location tracking, external requests | Location state, tax value, repair cost, and available route |
| Crypts | Rewind and route testing | What changed after rewind and whether knowledge created new choices |

## How to Decide the Next Building

| Current Problem | Build or Use First |
|---|---|
| Knights are dying or returning damaged | Forge, armor repair, and safer quest matching |
| You cannot cover Magic or strange quests | Witch / Alchemy support and Magic-focused knights |
| You are missing recruitment clues | Intendancy and knight profile tracking |
| Regions are damaged or income is unstable | Map / Gallery repair loop |
| You need to test a high-risk branch | Crypts and a written route log |

## Annex Data Format

Each building page should eventually use the same database-style fields:

| Field | Example Data to Record |
|---|---|
| Unlock condition | Story beat, resource cost, faction state, route choice, or recruitment count |
| Function | Repair, craft, reveal clue, unlock slot, open map route, or modify audience |
| Best timing | Early cycle, before Dragon Knight, before Act 2, or route-specific |
| Related systems | Knights, quests, meals, endings, Demon Power, or factions |
| Confidence | Confirmed, repeated, reported, or still unknown |

## FAQ

### Are annexes just upgrades?

No. Annexes affect route safety because they can reveal information, repair damage, support stats, unlock court positions, and change how prepared your knights are before a quest.

### Which annex should I build first?

Prioritize the annex that solves the failure you are actually seeing. For most risky routes, repair and information are stronger than blind expansion.

### Can annexes affect recruitment?

They may. Track whether a new annex opens a court slot, staff role, audience, or route scene before presenting it as a confirmed recruitment requirement.
