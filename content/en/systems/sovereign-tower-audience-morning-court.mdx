## Quick Answer

The morning audience is where many Sovereign Tower routes begin. Each petition can change faction values, spend money, create a quest, reveal a recruitment lead, or set up a later consequence. Record the ruling before assigning knights at the Round Table.

Use this page with the [Round Table System](/systems/sovereign-tower-round-table), [Factions Route](/quests/sovereign-tower-factions-route), and [All Quests](/quests).

## Audience Decision Table

| Decision Type | What to Track |
|---|---|
| Accept request | Quest name, deadline, faction impact, reward, and required follow-up |
| Refuse request | Faction loss, location damage, angered NPC, or missed quest |
| Spend money | Cost, treasury state, faction gain, and whether the result pays back later |
| Choose dialogue tone | Sovereign personality shift, affinity effect, and route text |
| Defer or investigate | Whether the request returns later with new information |

## Morning Court Workflow

1. Read the petitioner text before choosing the obvious reward.
2. Note every visible faction preview or cost.
3. Separate urgent route quests from low-risk errands.
4. Check whether the request creates a recruitment, romance, faction, or ending flag.
5. After the audience, assign accepted quests through the Round Table with the right knight.

## Faction Pressure Notes

| Pressure Pattern | Risk |
|---|---|
| Helping only one faction | Other groups may close quests, create rebellions, or damage income |
| Refusing costly public needs | People satisfaction may fall before major route checks |
| Spending too much on every audience | Treasury pressure can block gear, repairs, or annex upgrades |
| Choosing harsh dialogue repeatedly | Tyranny-style route state may affect later options |
| Choosing soft dialogue without resources | Kindness may not prevent practical failure if the quest still lacks support |

## FAQ

### Should I accept every audience request?

No. Accept requests that you can afford and complete safely. Refusing everything hurts factions, but accepting dangerous quests without the right knight can be worse.

### Do audience choices affect knight recruitment?

They can. Track any audience that introduces a knight, creates a route lead, opens a location, or changes loyalty before calling it a confirmed recruitment trigger.

### Can morning court choices affect endings?

Yes. Audience rulings can shift factions, personality, resources, and quest availability, all of which may affect ending routes.
