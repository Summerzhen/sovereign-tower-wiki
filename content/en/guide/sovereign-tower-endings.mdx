## How Endings Are Determined in Sovereign Tower

Sovereign Tower uses several interconnected systems to calculate which ending you receive. Based on player experience and the game's documented mechanics, your final outcome depends on a combination of the following factors:

- **Faction reputation** – How aligned you are with the various factions you encounter throughout each cycle

## Key Mechanics That Shape Your Ending

Understanding the core systems is critical if you want to steer toward a particular ending. Here's a breakdown of the mechanics that matter most.

### Faction Reputation

Faction reputation is introduced in Cycle 1 and remains a persistent force throughout the game. Every quest you accept, every knight you assign, and every dialogue option you select can raise or lower your standing with different factions. High reputation with a faction may open unique quests, unlock specific characters, or shift the narrative toward outcomes tied to that faction's goals.

### Knight Management and Quest Assignments

Your knights are more than party members—they're the tools you use to execute your will across the kingdom. Each knight has distinct stats, attributes, and traits that determine their suitability for specific quests.

| Knight | Key Trait | Quest Strength | Notable Weakness |
|--------|-----------|----------------|------------------|
| Angelica | Animal Lover | Animal-related quests (e.g., "Hammer Lost, Yet Again") | Not yet documented |
| Goberto | Hunting trait | Horde and hunting quests (e.g., "Face the Horde") | Low Agility |

Assigning the wrong knight to a quest can result in failure, which may ripple outward and affect faction reputation, character relationships, and ultimately your ending. For example, Goberto's low Agility causes him to fail certain missions unless you rewind time and reassign someone better suited.

### The Crypts and Time Rewind

Introduced in Cycle 2, the Crypts are one of the most important mechanics for players pursuing different Sovereign Tower endings. The Demon in the Crypts allows you to rewind to earlier cycles while retaining your knowledge and Archetype progress. This means you can:

- Correct failed quest assignments without starting over from scratch
- Explore different dialogue branches to see how they shift the story
- Build faction reputation along a new path
- Test alternate knight assignments for better quest outcomes

This mechanic is essentially the game's way of letting you chase multiple endings without requiring a completely fresh save each time.

## Strategy Guide: Pursuing the Ending You Want

Since Sovereign Tower features branching choices and multiple outcomes, you'll need a deliberate approach if you're targeting a specific ending. Here are practical strategies based on what the community has documented so far.

### 1. Track Faction Reputation Closely

Pay attention to how each quest and dialogue choice affects your standing with the game's factions. If you're aiming for a faction-aligned ending, prioritize their quests and assign knights who will succeed on those missions.

### 2. Match Knights to Quests Using Traits

Never assign a knight blindly. Review their traits and stats before sending them on a quest:

- **Angelica** excels at animal-related tasks thanks to her Animal Lover trait
- **Goberto** shines on hunting and horde-type quests due to his hunting trait, but avoid Agility-dependent missions
- New knights in later cycles may have traits that make them ideal for specific quest types

### 3. Use the Crypts Strategically

Don't rewind time at the first sign of trouble. Instead, use the Crypts after you've gathered enough information about which knight-quest combinations work best. Rewind with a plan, not out of panic.

### 4. Experiment with Dialogue Branches

Since the Crypts preserve your knowledge and Archetype progress, you can safely explore different dialogue options in earlier cycles. Take notes on which choices shift faction reputation or unlock new quest lines—this information is invaluable for steering toward your desired ending.

### 5. Don't Ignore Seemingly Small Choices

The Angelica cat decision is a perfect example. Small choices often reveal hidden traits or shift relationships in ways that compound over multiple cycles. Treat every decision as potentially ending-relevant.

Yes, based on player experience. Letting Angelica keep the cat reveals her Animal Lover trait, which directly affects her performance on certain quests. Small decisions in Sovereign Tower often have cascading effects on quest outcomes, faction reputation, and ultimately which ending you receive. Treat every choice as significant.
