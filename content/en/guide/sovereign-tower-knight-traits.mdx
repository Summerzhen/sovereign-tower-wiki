## Quick Answer

Knight traits are positive, negative, or hidden attributes that can change how a knight performs on quests. Revealing hidden traits gives you better assignment information before you commit to a cycle.

## Trait Types

| Trait Type | Use |
|---|---|
| Positive traits | Improve fitting quest outcomes |
| Negative traits | Create risk when the quest conflicts with the knight |
| Hidden traits | Require investigation, dialogue, or repeated-cycle knowledge |
| Preference traits | Help manage affinity, but may not decide success |

## How to Reveal Hidden Traits

Hidden traits can surface through relationship choices, dialogue, advisor investigation, or learning from a failed route and rewinding. Once revealed, record the trait on the knight page and link it to quests where it matters.

## Database Links to Build

Each trait should eventually connect:

- Knight pages that have the trait
- Quest pages where the trait matters
- Outcome notes that mention the trait
- Rewind strategies that use trait knowledge
