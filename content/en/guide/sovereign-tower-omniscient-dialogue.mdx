## What Is Omniscient Dialogue?

Omniscient Dialogue refers to special conversation options that can appear after the player has knowledge from another timeline or a previous run. Treat each option as a documented state change: record when it appears, what unlocked it, and what happens after choosing it.

## How to Track the Trigger

For every Omniscient Dialogue option, record:

1. The original scene and cycle.
2. Whether the player used time rewind, Demon Power, Reset, or New Game Plus.
3. The previous choice or quest outcome that supplied the knowledge.
4. The exact dialogue label and the resulting change.
5. Any effect on affinity, recruitment, quest outcome, or ending flags.

## Does It Change Quest Outcomes?

It may change the route, but individual outcomes should remain marked as unverified until the same state can be reproduced. Link the dialogue record to the affected quest and knight instead of publishing a universal recommendation from one run.

## Does It Affect Endings?

Track Omniscient Dialogue as an ending input when it changes a faction state, relationship, quest result, or late-game choice. The dialogue label alone is not enough evidence that it guarantees a specific ending.
