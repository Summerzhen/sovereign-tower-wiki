## Quick Answer

There is no fully confirmed universal best-knight tier list yet. Treat Sovereign Tower knights as role picks: match stats and traits to the quest, avoid loyalty losses you cannot repair, and track outcomes as confirmed data grows.

## Practical Tier List Framework

| Tier | What It Means | Best Use |
|---|---|---|
| S | Strong confirmed fit for a major quest type | High-stakes quests where failure is costly |
| A | Reliable when stats and traits match | Routine progression and route testing |
| B | Useful but situational | Low-risk assignments, affinity repair, experiments |
| Track | Promising but not enough confirmed data | Rewind testing and outcome logging |

## How to Pick the Best Knight

1. Read the quest requirement before looking at personality preference.
2. Match the highest relevant stat first.
3. Check traits for hidden upside, hidden danger, or unusual outcome text.
4. Consider loyalty and affinity loss only after the quest-success check.
5. Record the result so future tier placements can move from "track" to confirmed.

## Best-Knight Notes

| Knight Question | Safer Answer |

## FAQ

### Is Brunhilda the best knight?

Brunhilda should be evaluated by her confirmed route, stats, traits, and quest fit. Do not assume she is automatically best for every assignment.

### Can a low-tier knight still be worth using?

Yes. A situational knight can be excellent when the quest requirement, trait, and loyalty state line up.

### Will this tier list change?

Yes. Treat this page as a framework until more confirmed quest data, outcome logs, and knight-specific testing are available.
