## Quick Answer

The best opening strategy is to play the first cycles as a scouting route. Accept quests you can actually staff, keep armor safe, record faction changes, and use the Round Table to learn which knights fit which problem types before chasing a perfect ending.

Start with the [Complete Walkthrough](/guide/sovereign-tower-walkthrough), then use the [Quest Finder](/quests) whenever a specific quest appears.

## Opening Priority Order

| Priority | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Keep early knights alive | A death or departure shrinks your Round Table before the route opens up |
| Record audience choices | Morning court decisions create quests, costs, faction changes, and route flags |
| Match quests by stats first | Preferences matter, but visible requirements are the safest first filter |
| Preserve faction balance | Extreme faction swings can close later choices before you understand them |
| Unlock support rooms carefully | Forge, Intendancy, map, and alchemy support can prevent avoidable failures |

## First-Cycle Checklist

1. Write down every audience request before accepting too many.
2. Note visible cost, faction, and route-warning text.
3. Assign the safest quest first, not the flashiest one.
4. Avoid sending a damaged or poorly matched knight to a dangerous task.
5. Record outcomes before rewinding or continuing.

## Best Early Assignments

| Quest Pattern | Safer Opening Choice |
|---|---|
| Animal, forest, or gentle errand | Test Angelica-style agility, luck, and animal-friendly traits |
| Public court or noble pressure | Use high Charisma only if the knight's personality will not sabotage the scene |
| Combat, hunt, or monster problem | Check Strength, Agility, armor, and whether the knight likes dangerous work |
| Investigation or clue route | Prioritize Wit and Luck, then record whether hidden traits matter |
| Strange or supernatural event | Do not assume combat solves it; check Magic, Wit, and route warnings |

## What Not to Do Early

| Mistake | Better Opening Habit |
|---|---|
| Accepting every petition | Accept what your roster and money can support |
| Spending all money before seeing quest risk | Keep enough for repairs, gear, and urgent public needs |
| Using rewind without notes | Preserve the failed result as route knowledge first |
| Chasing recruitment rumors immediately | Confirm the scene, trigger, and route state before rerouting |
| Building around one favorite knight | Keep the roster flexible for different stat checks |

## FAQ

### Is there one best opening route?

Not yet. Treat the best opening as a reliable process: protect the roster, keep money flexible, learn quest requirements, and avoid irreversible assumptions.

### Should I use Demon Power early?

Use it to test, but write down the original failure first. The value of rewind is the information you carry forward.

### Should I recruit everyone as soon as possible?

Only if the route supports it. Recruitment can interact with roster capacity, loyalty, faction pressure, and quest access, so confirm the cost before forcing it.
