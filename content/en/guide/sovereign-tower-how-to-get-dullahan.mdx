## Quick Answer

Current public recruitment guides point to one main Dullahan route: **let Goberto die**. Dullahan then appears after Goberto's death rather than as a simple timed recruit. Treat this as a deliberate branch, not a casual upgrade, because it costs you Goberto and can affect later tournament, armour, and route planning.

Use a protected save before testing the route. Record which quest killed Goberto, his armour state, the cycle, and whether Dullahan appears immediately or after later audience progression in your current build.

## Who Is Dullahan in Sovereign Tower?

Dullahan introduces himself as "a human one" and goes out of his way to emphasize his organic nature. He claims to possess lungs, eyes, and a spinal column, and demonstrates his humanity by sorting boxes of hay and blinking—sometimes even twice per hour. If that sounds like someone trying a little too hard to convince you he's made of meat, you're not alone in your suspicion.

### The Dullahan Persona

Based on the official character introduction from the developers, Dullahan arrives at your court standing awkwardly, as if he's constantly trying to balance himself on perfectly flat ground. His speech is halting and deliberate, full of pauses and strange emphases that suggest he may not be entirely what he claims.

Key character traits include:

- Insists he is "totally organic" and "a human"
- Demonstrates human behavior in exaggerated, almost rehearsed ways
- Speaks in a slow, deliberate cadence with frequent pauses
- Expresses a desire for approval and to fulfill your desires
- Mentions being "so thirsty" at the end of his introduction

The name "Dullahan" itself is a clear reference to the headless horseman of Irish mythology—a type of fairy that rides through the night carrying its own head. Whether this naming is a hint at his true nature or a red herring remains to be seen, but it's hard to ignore the connection.

## Goberto vs. Dullahan: Route Cost

This is not a clean "choose one at the table" recruit. Current public data makes Dullahan a consequence of Goberto dying. That means the real question is whether your route can afford losing Goberto.

### Goberto of Naoned Profile

Goberto is an enthusiastic, boisterous knight from Naoned who arrives eager to prove himself. His key characteristics include:

- **Combat-focused.** He specializes in slaying wild beasts—trolls, dragons, giant spiders, furious goats, and even blood-thirsty chickens. If it moves, he wants to fight it.
- **Loyal and earnest.** Goberto openly expresses sympathy for your loneliness and wants to be a true companion, not just a soldier.
- **Ambitious.** His dream is to fight in the Great Tournament of Almor and hear crowds chant his name.
- **Straightforward.** What you see is what you get with Goberto—no hidden agendas or suspicious claims.

### Dullahan Profile

Dullahan is harder to read. His introduction raises more questions than it answers:

- **Uncertain nature.** His insistence on being human feels like a cover story. The awkward balancing, the rehearsed blinking, the hay-sorting—none of it feels natural.
- **Desire for approval.** Dullahan explicitly states he wants to fulfill your desires for approval, suggesting he may be deeply motivated by your favor.
- **Mysterious abilities.** The introduction doesn't showcase any combat skills, leaving his actual utility unclear.
- **Potential hidden depth.** Games rarely introduce a character this strange without payoff. Dullahan's oddness could signal unique mechanics or story beats later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take for Dullahan to appear in Sovereign Tower?

Current public recruitment data points to Goberto's death as the trigger, not a simple fixed calendar wait. After Goberto dies, keep checking court and Round Table events for Dullahan's arrival.

### Can you recruit both Goberto and Dullahan, or do you have to choose one?

Do not plan around having both in a normal route. Public guides currently connect Dullahan to Goberto being dead.

### Is Dullahan actually human in Sovereign Tower?

Dullahan claims to be "a human one" who is "totally organic," but his behavior strongly suggests otherwise. His name references the headless horseman of Irish mythology, his movements are described as awkward and unbalanced, and his attempts to prove his humanity—like blinking twice per hour and sorting hay—feel rehearsed and unnatural. Most players suspect he is not what he claims to be, but no official confirmation has been released yet.

### What happens if you miss Dullahan's recruitment in Sovereign Tower?

If Goberto is still alive, you probably have not triggered the route. If Goberto died and Dullahan does not appear, record the quest, cycle, armour state, and whether Goberto was truly removed from the roster before retrying.
