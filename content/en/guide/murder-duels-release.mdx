## What Is Murder Duels? A New Roblox Shooter Explained

Murder Duels is a brand-new deathmatch shooter on Roblox, and the Murder Duels release has already pulled in over 118 million visits. Created by Rip Indra—the developer behind Blox Fruits—this game trades fruit-powered combat for fast-paced knife-and-revolver duels where the first player to five kills takes the match. Whether you're curious about how duels work, what abilities can turn the tide, or whether saving up for a Founder's Case is worth it, this guide breaks down everything we know so far.

Because the game is still in beta, expect frequent changes. The developer has already pushed a Summer Update in two parts, showing that active support is a priority.

## How Duels Work: Modes, Rules, and Match Flow

Getting into a match in Murder Duels is straightforward. You step on a pad in the lobby to queue up, and the game matches you against other players. The core ruleset is simple: the first player or team to reach five round wins claims the overall victory.

### Available Duel Modes

| Mode | Players | Description |
|------|---------|-------------|
| 1v1 | 2 total | Classic one-on-one duel |
| 2v2 | 4 total | Paired team fights |
| 3v3 | 6 total | Larger squad battles |
| 4v4 | 8 total | Biggest team mode available |

Based on player experience, 1v1 matches are the easiest to find since they require fewer players. Larger modes like 3v3 and 4v4 can have longer queue times because the lobby needs more participants to fill both sides. If you're trying to grind wins quickly, sticking to 1v1 or 2v2 is generally the faster route.

### Match Structure

Each duel consists of multiple rounds. When you die in a round, you're out until the next one begins. The victory screen displays between rounds, giving you a moment to check your loadout or adjust your strategy. You can also spectate live games while waiting, which is a great way to study how experienced players position themselves and use abilities.

## Weapons and Abilities: Knives, Revolvers, and Skills

Every duel equips you with two weapons: a knife and a revolver. Both are essential, and knowing when to switch between them can decide the outcome of a fight.

### Knife

The knife serves double duty as a melee and ranged tool. You can slash opponents up close or throw it for ranged damage. Player reports indicate that throwing the knife can even hit multiple enemies with collateral damage. One of the most important knife mechanics is that switching to your knife is faster than reloading your revolver, making it a reliable backup when you run out of ammo mid-fight.

### Revolver

The revolver is your primary ranged weapon. It deals solid damage but has a reload time that leaves you vulnerable. The key to using the revolver effectively is landing your shots before the enemy closes the gap or tags you with a thrown knife.

### Weapon Abilities

Both weapons come with a set of unique abilities that give you an edge in combat. Based on community reports and gameplay footage, here are some of the abilities players have encountered:

- **Wall Hack**: Lets you see enemy positions through walls, giving you a major informational advantage
- **Super Jump**: Allows you to reach elevated positions and escape dangerous angles
- **Explosive Knife**: Adds area-of-effect damage to your thrown knife, making it deadlier in tight spaces
- **Gun Ability**: A special revolver skill, though player feedback suggests it may not always feel as impactful as knife abilities

| Ability | Weapon | Effect | Best Used For |
|---------|--------|--------|---------------|
| Wall Hack | Either | See enemies through walls | Tracking opponents in 1v1 |
| Super Jump | Either | Reach high ground | Repositioning and escapes |
| Explosive Knife | Knife | AoE damage on throw | Group fights in 2v2+ |
| Gun Ability | Revolver | Special ranged skill | Situational ranged pressure |

Choosing the right ability for your playstyle can make a noticeable difference. If you prefer an aggressive approach, Super Jump and Explosive Knife reward pushing into fights. If you like to play methodically, Wall Hack gives you the information to set up ambushes.

## Skins, Cases, and Trading: The Progression System

Murder Duels features a skin system for both knives and revolvers. As you play and win matches, you'll earn currency that can be spent on cases containing cosmetic skins of varying rarity.

### Case Types and Rarity

The game offers at least two types of cases:

- **Standard Case**: Lower cost, but skewed toward lower-rarity drops
- **Founder's Case**: Costs 4,000 currency, presumably offering better odds for rare skins

Based on player experience, the standard case has a high chance—around 71%—of dropping an uncommon skin. This means if you're hoping for a rare or better cosmetic, you might want to save up for the Founder's Case instead of gambling on cheaper options repeatedly.

One of the standout features of the Murder Duels release is the ability to trade skins with other players. If you pull a duplicate or a skin you don't want, you can exchange it with someone else. ## Tips for Winning More Duels

Winning consistently in Murder Duels requires more than just good aim. Here are practical strategies pulled from player experience and gameplay observations:

- **Switch to your knife instead of reloading**. The swap is faster, and a thrown knife can finish off a low-health opponent before they can react.
- **Use Wall Hack to pre-aim corners**. Knowing where your enemy is before they peek gives you a massive advantage in 1v1 situations.
- **Save your currency for the Founder's Case**. The odds of getting something worthwhile from standard cases are low, and a rare skin could be a great trade chip.
- **Spectate top players on the leaderboard**. Watching how high-ranked players move, aim, and use abilities is one of the fastest ways to improve.
- **Be cautious around corners in team modes**. In 2v2 and larger modes, enemies can approach from unexpected angles. Keep moving and don't stay still for too long.
- **Learn the maps**. Some areas have bright lighting or tight corridors that can affect visibility and movement. Familiarity with map layouts helps you avoid getting caught off guard.
- **Don't underestimate mobile players**. Community reports indicate that mobile players can be surprisingly competitive, so never let your guard down.

| Group of enemies clustered | Throw explosive knife for AoE damage |
| Just started playing | Stick to 1v1 for faster matches and practice |
| Have 4,000+ currency | Buy Founder's Case instead of standard cases |

## Badges and Achievements

Murder Duels currently features at least two badges that players can earn:

- **Welcome to Murder Duels!**: Earned by winning a match. This is your first milestone and proves you can hold your own in a duel.
- **ACE Badge**: Earned by getting an ACE, which means killing every player on the opposing team in a single round by yourself during a 3v3 or 4v4 match. This is significantly harder and rewards truly dominant performances.

The ACE badge is a long-term goal for most players. You'll need strong mechanical skills, good ability usage, and a bit of luck with positioning to pull it off. Focus on improving in smaller modes first before chasing this achievement.
