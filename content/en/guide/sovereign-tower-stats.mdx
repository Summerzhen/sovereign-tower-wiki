## Quick Answer

Sovereign Tower uses six major knight stats: Strength, Agility, Charisma, Luck, Magic, and Wit. When choosing a quest assignment, identify the required stat first, then compare traits, armor, equipment, and affinity.

## Stat Reference

| Stat | Best For |
|---|---|
| Strength | Fighting, duels, heavy labor, direct danger |
| Agility | Retrieval, dodging, climbing, speed |
| Charisma | Diplomacy, persuasion, courtly situations |
| Luck | Swingy outcomes, risky quests, unusual twists |
| Magic | Supernatural threats, relics, spell-linked tasks |
| Wit | Investigation, puzzles, clever solutions |

## Assignment Order

1. Read the quest text.
2. Find the required stat or strongest clue.
3. Pick knights whose stats match.
4. Check visible and hidden traits.
5. Consider armor, equipment, and damage risk.
6. Use affinity as the final tiebreaker.
