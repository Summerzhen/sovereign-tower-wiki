## Quick Answer

Unexpected outcomes are unusual quest results that do not behave like a normal success or failure. They can be triggered by stat matches, hidden traits, risky assignments, special choices, or information learned through time rewind.

Use the [All Quests database](/quests) first when you know the quest name. Use this page when you are comparing why the same quest produced a different result after changing the knight, trait match, route state, or rewind knowledge.

## High-Priority Outcome Tests

| Quest / Route | Why It Should Be Tested | What to Compare |
|---|---|---|
| Dragon Knight | Major cycle-four pressure point with roster, armor, faction, and rewind consequences | Four-knight roster state, People satisfaction, money, armor, and post-failure Demon Power text |
| Gavault | Early Act 1 route likely to affect recruitment, factions, and regional branches | Same quest with different charisma, wits, and public-order choices |
| Groveshire | Region route connected to wilderness, animal-friendly, and recruitment questions | Angelica-style traits, Rufus route notes, and forest-choice outcomes |
| Beast Hunt | Combat route where the wrong knight can create damage or failure data | Strength/agility match, armor loss, and whether beast traits change the result |
| Goose Quest | Odd quest chain with strong "why did this happen" player intent | Normal success, strange result, knight personality, and choice wording |
| Rebellion | Faction-sensitive route where outcomes may affect later route locks | People/Nobles balance, loyalty state, and failed negotiation consequences |

## Fast Diagnosis Table

| If the Result Changed | Check This First |
|---|---|
| Same knight, different result | Route state, faction state, Demon Power knowledge, or prior audience choice |
| Different knight, different result | Required stats, visible traits, hidden traits, favorite/ hated quest type |
| Success became critical success | Trait match, high relevant stat, equipment boost, or affinity state |
| Success became unexpected outcome | Special trait, unusual route flag, risky choice, or secret condition |
| Failure became severe failure | Low armor, wrong stat, hostile trait match, or ignored loyalty warning |

## Outcome Types to Track

| Outcome | What to Record |
|---|---|
| Critical success | Required stats, knight, trait, reward |
| Standard success | Basic completion and reward |
| Failure | Missing requirement, damage, affinity loss |
| Critical failure | Severe loss, death, route damage |
| Unexpected outcome | Trigger condition, story result, whether it is good or bad |

## Why This Page Matters

Players will search for better outcomes once they learn a quest can branch. A single outcome guide can collect broad intent, then link to individual quest pages as the database grows.

## Unexpected Outcomes Database Format

Use the same fields for every documented quest so players can compare normal and special results without mixing confirmed data with route reports.

| Quest | Normal result | Special knight or trait | Unexpected outcome | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Record quest | Success / Failure | Record exact trigger | Record in-game result | Unverified until repeated |

Filter the [All Quests database](/quests) by outcome, then open the individual quest record for the full requirement and knight context.

## Rewind Strategy

When you hit an unexpected outcome, do not immediately erase it. Write down the knight, stats, known traits, quest text, and choice path first. Then rewind with a specific test in mind.
