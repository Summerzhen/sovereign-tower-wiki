## Quick Answer

Sovereign Tower quest outcomes are not just pass or fail messages. They can change rewards, armor damage, affinity, faction state, recruitment leads, future quests, and ending routes. Record the knight, stats, traits, equipment, and route state whenever a result surprises you.

Use the [Quest Finder](/quests) to open the quest record, then compare this page with [Unexpected Outcomes](/guide/sovereign-tower-unexpected-outcomes) for hidden or unusual results.

## Outcome Types

| Outcome | What It Usually Means |
|---|---|
| Critical Success | The knight exceeded the requirement or matched the quest unusually well |
| Success | The task completed without major route damage |
| Failure | The assignment missed a requirement, trait fit, or route condition |
| Critical Failure | The result caused serious damage, death risk, faction harm, or route loss |
| Unexpected Outcome | A hidden trait, strange choice, or route flag changed the expected result |

## What to Record After Every Quest

| Field | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Assigned knight | Lets you compare stats, traits, loyalty, and preferences |
| Required stats | Shows whether the visible requirement explains the result |
| Equipment / support | Gear, potions, meals, and annex support may shift the threshold |
| Armor and damage | A surviving success can still create future death risk |
| Faction changes | Some outcomes matter because they move political state |
| Follow-up event | Rewards, audiences, recruitment clues, and route locks often appear later |

## Outcome Diagnosis

| If You Saw This | Check This First |
|---|---|
| Success but low reward | Missing trait match, low affinity, or no critical threshold |
| Failure with good stats | Disliked quest type, hidden trait conflict, loyalty issue, or wrong route state |
| Critical failure | Armor, danger warning, missing key stat, or forced bad assignment |
| Unexpected result | Hidden trait, special dialogue, prior quest, or rewind knowledge |
| Follow-up disappeared | Different audience choice, faction value, recruitment state, or timing |

## Rewind Testing Method

1. Save the original outcome in your notes.
2. Change one variable: knight, equipment, dialogue, or quest order.
3. Run the quest again and compare only that variable.
4. Add the result to the quest record when it repeats.

## FAQ

### Are unexpected outcomes always good?

No. An unexpected outcome can be a hidden benefit, a strange complication, or a route warning. Treat it as information before deciding whether to rewind.

### Can a normal success still be bad?

Yes. A quest can succeed while damaging armor, lowering affinity, spending too much money, or pushing a faction in the wrong direction.

### Should every outcome get its own page?

No. Keep outcomes in the quest database first. Split a separate page only when players search for that exact outcome or the route consequence is large.
