## Quick Answer

Recruit knights by progressing their story access, completing relevant quests, choosing supportive dialogue, and tracking any loyalty or roster conditions. Avoid claiming exact triggers until the route has confirmed data.

## Recruitment Factors

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Story progress | Some knights may only appear after specific route beats |
| Quest outcome | Success, failure, or unexpected outcomes can change availability |
| Dialogue | Trust-building choices may affect recruitment or loyalty |
| Loyalty | Low loyalty can create departure risk or block progress |
| Roster state | Existing knights may affect scenes, conflicts, or options |

## How to Recruit More Safely

1. Build a route log for every new knight encounter.
2. Note the quest and location where the knight appears.
3. Record dialogue choices that produce relationship feedback.
4. Finish linked quests with the best stat and trait match available.
5. Confirm the exact join condition before calling it a requirement.

## FAQ

### Can every knight be recruited in one route?

Do not assume that until route conflicts and roster limits are confirmed.

### Does affinity affect recruitment?

It may. Track affinity and loyalty when testing recruitment because relationship state can affect availability in many RPG systems.

### Should I prioritize recruitment over quest success?

Only when the quest is low risk. For important quests, secure the outcome first and repair affinity later when possible.
