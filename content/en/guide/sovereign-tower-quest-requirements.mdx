## Quick Answer

Read quest requirements before assigning knights. Sovereign Tower quests can depend on stats, traits, party size, equipment, damage risk, and hidden outcome triggers.

## Requirement Checklist

| Requirement | What to Check |
|---|---|
| Stat | Strength, Agility, Charisma, Luck, Magic, or Wit |
| Trait | Positive, negative, hidden, or preference-linked |
| Risk | Armor damage, injury, failure, or death |
| Party | Whether one knight is enough or support is needed |
| Outcome | Normal success, critical success, failure, or unexpected outcome |

## Best Assignment Flow

1. Open the quest.
2. Identify the strongest stat clue.
3. Compare all available knights.
4. Check known traits and hidden trait notes.
5. Review armor and equipment.
6. Assign the safest capable knight.
7. Record the result for future cycles.
