## Fields Needed for Every Knight

| Field | Required Before Calling It Complete |
|---|---|
| Name and title | Exact in-game display name or official source wording |
| Role | Starter, recruitable, secret, route-only, NPC, or unknown |
| Stats | Strength, Agility, Charisma, Luck, Magic, Wit, and armor when visible |
| Traits | Visible traits plus hidden traits only after they are revealed in-game |
| Favorite meals | Confirmed meal names, not guessed affinity assumptions |
| Recruitment | Act, cycle, audience, quest, route, or condition that unlocks the knight |
| Best quests | At least one assignment where stats, traits, and outcome are documented |
| Evolution | Evolution name, requirements, stat/trait changes, and confidence level |

## FAQ

### Why not add all 14 missing profile pages immediately?

Because names alone are not enough. Each new profile should include at least one useful gameplay field: recruitment, stats, traits, favorite meals, evolution, best quests, or route consequences.

### When should a roster name become a database record?

Create a record when the knight has a stable name plus at least one useful gameplay field: stats, recruitment scene, favorite meal, trait, or quest assignment.

### Should uncertain knights stay in the database?

Only if the page clearly labels uncertainty. If a name may be a creature, route symbol, NPC, or community shorthand, the profile should say that plainly.
