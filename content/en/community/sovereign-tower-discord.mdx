## Getting the Most Out of the Community

### Tips for New Sovereigns

If you're just starting your reign, the Discord community can help you avoid early pitfalls. Here's what experienced players recommend focusing on:

- **Knight personalities matter**: Each Knight has unique traits suited to different quests. Diplomatic missions need different Knights than tournaments
- **Don't fear the rewind**: The time travel system is one of the game's strongest features according to PC Gamer. Use it to explore alternate outcomes
- **Feed your Knights**: Raising affection through food choices can unlock new story paths
- **Watch for eyestrain scenes**: Community reports indicate some flashing and glitch effects that may cause discomfort

### When to Ask for Help vs. Explore Alone

| Situation | Recommended Approach |
|---|---|
| Stuck on a Knight's quest for 30+ minutes | Ask in Discord—someone has likely solved it |
| Unsure about a major story branch | Use spoiler channels to weigh options |
| Achievement not unlocking | Check community guides first, then ask |
| Wondering about hidden Knights | Spoiler channel discussions can point you in the right direction |
| General gameplay questions | Open channels welcome basic questions |

The game encourages multiple playthroughs, so don't feel pressured to optimize everything on your first run. Sometimes the most memorable moments come from living with your mistakes—until you decide to rewrite fate.
