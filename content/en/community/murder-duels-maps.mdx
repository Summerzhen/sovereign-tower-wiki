## Key Map Features to Learn

Every Murder Duels map shares certain structural elements. Recognizing these patterns helps you adapt quickly, even to arenas you have not memorized.

### Common Layout Elements

- **Spawn zones**: Where players appear at the start of a round. These areas tend to be relatively open, giving everyone a moment to orient before the murderer is revealed.
- **Corridors and hallways**: Narrow passages that are high-risk for survivors. The murderer can close distance fast here, but a skilled gun user can also catch the murderer in a choke point.
- **Open courtyards or plazas**: Wider spaces where guns have the advantage. If you are holding a ranged weapon, position yourself with clear sightlines.
- **Elevated positions**: Balconies, rooftops, or platforms that offer vantage points. These are powerful for gun users but dangerous if the murderer can flank you.
- **Hiding spots**: Corners, behind objects, or inside rooms with one entrance. Useful early in a round but risky if the murderer checks methodically.

| Feature | Priority for Survivors | Priority for Murderer |
|---|---|---|
| Spawn zones | Medium – move away quickly | High – track early movement |
| Corridors | Low – avoid if possible | High – prime ambush territory |
| Open areas | High – best for gun fights | Low – too exposed |
| Elevated spots | High – great vantage points | Medium – flank routes exist |
| Hiding spots | Low – temporary safety only | High – check systematically |

## Skills and How They Interact with Maps

Murder Duels features skills that add depth to gameplay. Two notable skills—X-Ray and Decoy—have particularly strong interactions with map layouts.

### X-Ray Skill

The X-Ray skill lets you see through walls or obstacles for a limited time. On maps with lots of corridors and rooms, X-Ray is extremely powerful. You can spot the murderer approaching before they round a corner, or locate survivors if you are the one hunting.

**Best maps for X-Ray**: Complex, multi-room arenas where line of sight is frequently blocked.

### Decoy Skill

The Decoy skill creates a false target that can mislead other players. On larger, more open maps, a decoy placed in a visible area can draw fire from gun users or lure the murderer away from your real position.

**Best maps for Decoy**: Open arenas where players rely on visual identification at a distance.

### Skill-Map Pairing Recommendations

| Skill | Map Type | Effectiveness | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| X-Ray | Enclosed, multi-room | Very High | Walls become transparent; information advantage is massive |
| X-Ray | Open, outdoor | Medium | Fewer walls to see through; less impactful |
| Decoy | Open, outdoor | High | Decoy visible from far away; draws attention effectively |
| Decoy | Enclosed, tight | Low-Medium | Players are close enough to quickly identify the fake |

## Strategies for Different Murder Duels Maps

Since maps rotate and new ones can appear with updates, the best approach is to develop flexible strategies rather than memorizing a single layout. Here is how to adapt your playstyle based on what you see.

### Strategy for Enclosed Maps

Enclosed maps are the murderer's playground. If you are a survivor:

- **Keep moving**. Standing still in a room with one exit is a death sentence.
- **Stick near gun-wielding teammates**. There is safety in numbers when sightlines are short.
- **Use X-Ray if available**. Knowing where the murderer is before they enter your hallway saves lives.
- **Avoid dead-end rooms**. Always have an escape route planned.

If you are the murderer:

- **Patrol corridors**. Survivors have to pass through them eventually.
- **Use corners and doorways**. Ambush from just out of sight.
- **Listen for movement cues**. Tight maps mean you will hear players nearby.

### Strategy for Open Maps

Open maps shift the balance toward survivors with guns. If you are a survivor:

- **Claim elevated ground**. Height gives you a better angle and more time to react.
- **Watch the murderer's approach**. In open space, you can see them coming from farther away.
- **Use Decoy to create confusion**. Place it where it will be spotted, then reposition.
- **Stay with at least one other player**. A lone survivor is easier to isolate.

If you are the murderer:

- **Use cover to close distance**. Rushing across open ground gets you shot.
- **Target isolated players first**. Pick off stragglers before engaging groups.
- **Flank using map edges**. Going around the perimeter keeps you out of sightlines.

### Strategy for Mixed Layouts

Many Murder Duels maps combine open areas with tight corridors. These arenas reward adaptability.

- **Transition between areas deliberately**. Move from open to enclosed spaces based on your role.
- **Memorize connector points**. Doors and passageways between zones are high-traffic and high-risk.
- **Adjust your skill choice**. If the map has both open and tight sections, choose the skill that complements the zone where you spend the most time.

## Tips for Learning New Maps Quickly

When a new map drops—often alongside a seasonal update—you want to get comfortable fast. Here are practical steps to speed up the learning process.

### First Few Rounds on a New Map

- **Play survivor first**. You will have more time to observe the layout while others take the lead.
- **Walk, do not run**. Moving slowly lets you absorb details about rooms, corridors, and exits.
- **Follow experienced players**. Watch where they go and how they position themselves.
- **Note the spawn area**. Every round starts here, so understanding its connections to the rest of the map is essential.

### Building Map Mastery

- **Play both roles**. Understanding a map as the murderer and as a survivor reveals different strategic angles.
- **Test skill interactions**. Try X-Ray and Decoy on the same map to see which works better for your playstyle.
- **Replay the map multiple times in a row**. Repetition builds muscle memory for routes and hiding spots.
- **Watch community content**. Other players may discover shortcuts or strategies you missed.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Ignoring the minimap or environmental cues that hint at room connections.
- Over-relying on a single hiding spot. Good murderers check every corner.
- Forgetting that map layouts can change with updates. What worked last month may not work now.
- Skipping skill unlocks. X-Ray and Decoy are game-changers on the right map.
