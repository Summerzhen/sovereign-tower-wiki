## How to Access the In-Game Shop

Getting to the shop in Murder Duels is simple, but there are two different methods depending on your preference. Both lead to the same interface, so pick whichever feels more natural.

### Method One: The Shop Button

When you load into Murder Duels, you spawn in the main lobby. Look at the left side of your screen and click the shop button. This instantly opens the shop menu without making your character move. It is the fastest option and works well if you already know what you want.

### Method Two: The Physical Shop Counter

If you prefer a more immersive approach, walk your character over to the physical shop counter located inside the lobby. Interacting with the counter pulls up the same shop menu. Some players enjoy this method because it feels more connected to the game world.

| Access Method | Steps | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Shop Button | 1. Load into lobby 2. Click shop button on left side | Quick browsing, returning players |
| Physical Counter | 1. Load into lobby 2. Walk to counter 3. Interact | New players exploring the lobby |

Both methods take you to the same screen, so you never miss out on items regardless of how you get there.

## Understanding Cases in the Murder Duels Shop

Cases are the primary way to unlock new weapons and cosmetics in the game. Once you open the shop menu, click on the cases tab to see what is available. Based on community reports, there are at least two primary case types that players encounter early on.

### The Recruit Case

The recruit case is designed with beginners in mind. It offers a lower-cost entry point and typically contains more common items. If you are just starting out, this case gives you a solid foundation of weapons and cosmetics without draining your currency. Think of it as your starter pack for building a respectable inventory.

### Higher-Tier Cases

Beyond the recruit case, the shop features premium options with rarer rewards. These cases cost more but offer a chance at legendary and event-exclusive items. The tradeoff is straightforward: spend more for a shot at better loot, or stick with budget cases for consistent but less exciting drops.

| Case Type | Cost Level | Rarity Range | Recommended For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruit Case | Low | Common to Uncommon | New players, budget shoppers |
| Premium Cases | Medium to High | Uncommon to Legendary | Experienced players, collectors |

Keep in mind that case contents are randomized. There is no guaranteed drop from any single case, so set a spending limit before you start opening them.

## Smart Shopping Tips for the Murder Duels Shop

Whether you are spending in-game currency or real money on a marketplace, a few strategies help you get more value from every purchase.

### Set a Budget Before You Shop

It is easy to get carried away opening cases or chasing a specific item. Decide how much you are willing to spend before you start, and stick to that number. This applies to both in-game currency and real-money transactions.

### Prioritize Items That Match Your Playstyle

A flashy gun skin is only worth it if you actually enjoy using that weapon type. Focus on items for loadouts you play regularly. This ensures your purchases enhance your experience rather than sitting unused in your inventory.

### Compare Prices Across Sources

Before buying a specific item on a marketplace, check if it is available through the in-game shop or as a case drop. Sometimes patience pays off, and you can get the same item for less by waiting for a shop rotation.

### Watch for Sales and Event Rotations

Community reports indicate that the in-game shop rotates its offerings periodically. Items that are unavailable today might return at a discount during a seasonal event. Keep an eye on community forums and update logs for announcements.

### Check Seller Reputation on Marketplaces

If you use a third-party platform, always review the seller's rating and order history. Sellers with high ratings and thousands of completed orders are generally more reliable than new or unrated listings.
