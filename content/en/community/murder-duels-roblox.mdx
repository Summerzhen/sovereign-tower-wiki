## What Is Murder Duels on Roblox?

Murder Duels Roblox blends the social deduction tension of Murder Mystery 2 with the competitive gunplay of Rivals, wrapping it all into a fast-paced arena shooter. Players step onto a pad, grab a knife and revolver, and fight to five wins across multiple team sizes. The game is still in beta, but it has already pulled over 118 million visits, making it one of the hotter new shooters on the platform.

If you enjoy skill-based PvP where every shot and ability matters, this guide breaks down everything you need to start winning consistently.

## Getting Started: How Duels Work

### Joining a Match

When you enter a server, you will see pads on the ground representing different duel sizes. Simply step on a pad to queue for that format. The match begins once enough players have joined. According to the [official Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/120851538706364/Murder-Duels), the first player or team to reach five round wins takes the match.

### Basic Rules

- Every player spawns with two weapons: a **knife** and a **revolver**.
- Both weapons come with **unique abilities** that can be activated during combat.
- Rounds are quick—positioning, aim, and ability usage determine the winner.
- The game is currently in **beta**, so mechanics and balance may change over time.

## Weapons and Abilities Breakdown

The standout feature that separates Murder Duels from similar Roblox shooters is the ability system. Both your knife and revolver have unlockable skills that change how you approach each fight.

### Revolver

Your revolver is your primary ranged tool. Community reports indicate that damage can vary—some players have noted hits dealing around 24 to 75 damage depending on whether the shot is a body shot or headshot. Headshots appear to deal significantly more damage, so aiming high is rewarded.

Key revolver behaviors observed by players:

- **No-scoping** is possible but inconsistent at longer ranges.
- Shots can sometimes fail to register on moving targets, likely due to the game's beta netcode.
- You can shoot through certain surfaces like glass on specific maps.

### Knife

The knife serves as your close-quarters option, but certain knife abilities turn it into a ranged threat as well. The most notable ability discovered by players is the **Explosive Knife**, which throws and detonates on impact for area damage. Many players consider this ability extremely strong—some even call it overpowered.

### Abilities

Abilities can be purchased and equipped between rounds. One example found in the game's skill shop is **Speed Surge**, which bursts you forward with increased movement speed. Other observed abilities include:

- **Smoke Bomb** – deploys a cloud that obscures vision.
- **Explosive Knife** – your knife throw detonates for splash damage.
- **Grenade-type tools** – some players have reported opponents using grenade-launching swords and throwable explosives.

## Duel Formats: Choosing the Right Mode

Murder Duels offers four team sizes, each requiring a different approach.

### 1v1 – Pure Skill

The 1v1 format strips away team dynamics. It is just you, your aim, and your ability usage against a single opponent. This mode is ideal for practicing mechanics, but queue times can be longer since fewer players choose it.

**Tips for 1v1:**
- Learn the movement system early—sliding and jumping are critical for dodging.
- Save your ability for a decisive moment rather than using it on cooldown.
- Aim for headshots to maximize your revolver damage.

### 2v2 – Balanced Teamwork

The 2v2 format is the sweet spot for most players. You have a teammate to trade fire with, but individual skill still carries significant weight. Communication and crossfire are key.

**Tips for 2v2:**
- Play off your teammate—peek opposite angles to split the enemy's attention.
- If your teammate goes down, play defensively and force a 1v1 on your terms.
- Use explosive or area abilities when enemies group together.

### 3v3 and 4v4 – Chaos and Coordination

Larger formats introduce more variables. One highly skilled player can dominate, but coordinated teams will usually win. The ACE badge—awarded for killing the entire opposing team in a single round by yourself in 3v3 or 4v4—is a rare achievement that highlights exceptional play.

**Tips for larger formats:**
- Focus fire on one target at a time to quickly reduce enemy numbers.
- Watch the scoreboard—players with high win counts are priority targets.
- Area-of-effect abilities like the Explosive Knife shine when enemies cluster.

| 2v2 | 4 | Moderate | Crossfire and trading |

## Maps and Environmental Hazards

Map knowledge gives you a serious edge. While the full map rotation is still being documented by the community, a few notable maps have been identified through gameplay.

### Temple

Temple is a fan-favorite with elevated positions, cover, and open sightlines. Controlling the high ground gives you a clear revolver advantage, but you become an easy target for explosive abilities if you stay still too long.

### Corners

As the name suggests, Corners emphasizes tight angles and close-quarters engagements. Knife abilities and Speed Surge are especially useful here since fights happen at close range.

### Environmental Hazards

- **Water** – Touching water on certain maps reduces your HP, leaving you vulnerable to a single shot. Avoid it unless the risk is worth the positional advantage.
- **Glass surfaces** – Some barriers are transparent and can be shot through. Do not assume cover is safe just because you cannot be seen clearly.

## Skins, Trading, and Progression

### Unlocking Skins

As you win matches, you unlock new knife and revolver skins. These are cosmetic and do not affect gameplay, but they serve as visible proof of your experience level. Other players will often check your loadout and win count before a match begins.

### Trading

Murder Duels includes a skin trading system, allowing you to exchange cosmetics with other players. This adds a collectible meta-game on top of the PvP action. Details on trade values are still emerging from the community, but rare skins from higher win milestones tend to be more desirable.

### Badges

The game features at least two badges:

| Badge | How to Earn |
|-------|-------------|
| Welcome to Murder Duels! | Win a match |
| ACE | Kill all opposing players in a single round of 3v3 or 4v4 by yourself |

## Tips for New Players

Starting out in Murder Duels Roblox can feel overwhelming, especially when matched against players who also grind similar games like Rivals. Here are practical strategies to shorten the learning curve:

- **Spend your first few games in 2v2.** You get a teammate who can compensate for mistakes while you learn the maps and mechanics.
- **Equip the Explosive Knife as soon as you can.** It provides ranged damage with your melee slot and is one of the strongest abilities currently available.
- **Practice movement before aim.** Sliding, jumping, and using Speed Surge to dodge are more important than landing every shot when you are new.
- **Avoid water and environmental hazards.** One mistake can leave you at low HP for the entire round.
- **Watch players with high win counts.** Observe how they position, when they use abilities, and how they approach gunfights. You can learn more from one match against a skilled opponent than from ten easy wins.

## FAQ

### Is Murder Duels Roblox free to play?

Yes, Murder Duels is free to play on Roblox. You can join matches, earn skins, and trade without spending Robux. Cosmetic purchases may exist in the game's store, but they are optional.

### What is the best ability in Murder Duels?

Based on community reports, the **Explosive Knife** is widely considered the strongest ability currently available. It adds area damage to your knife throw, making it effective in all duel formats. Speed Surge is also valuable for players who struggle with positioning and movement.

### How do I get the ACE badge in Murder Duels Roblox?

The ACE badge is earned by killing every player on the opposing team in a single round during a 3v3 or 4v4 match—all by yourself. This means your teammates must not get any kills that round. It is a rare achievement that requires both skill and favorable circumstances.

### Is Murder Duels better than MM2?

That depends on what you want from the experience. Murder Duels focuses on competitive PvP with weapon abilities and multiple team formats, while Murder Mystery 2 emphasizes social deduction and role-based gameplay. Players who prefer fast-paced shooter mechanics tend to favor Murder Duels, while those who enjoy mystery and deception may prefer MM2.
