## Recommended Approach

Start Groveshire by building a balanced early court. Do not level every knight in the same direction; keep at least one physical specialist, one social specialist, and one flexible assignment option.

Before ending a cycle, ask three questions:

1. Which stat does this quest actually ask for?
2. Which knight has the best matching stat and trait profile?
3. What will I learn if I need to rewind?
