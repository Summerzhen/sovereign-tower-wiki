## Quick Answer

For the Factions route, keep a running ledger of every decision that changes approval with Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles, the People, or Treasury. The important question is not which faction is universally best, but which commitments create stable access for your current route.

## Faction Overview

| Route Point | Working Plan |
|---|---|
| Primary goal | Understand which faction states open, alter, or close quests and audiences |
| Planning stats | Charisma, Wit, and Strength are in-game planning notes for negotiation, schemes, and enforcement |
| Suggested anchors | Gwendan, Brunhilda, and Dullahan are useful when their visible fit matches the event |
| Best traits | Court-facing, route knowledge, battle-ready, and stable loyalty |
| Main risk | Over-favoring one group before knowing what the neglected factions control |

## Steps / Route Fit

1. Create a faction ledger before each major quest or audience decision.
2. Record the starting state, decision text, assigned knight, outcome, and any approval change shown by the game.
3. Separate immediate approval changes from delayed consequences such as rebellion pressure or blocked recruitment.
4. Compare faction outcomes with [Alliances Route](/quests/sovereign-tower-alliances-route), [Rebellion](/quests/sovereign-tower-rebellion), and the full campaign route.
5. Use balanced-state saves as control points before testing extreme alignment paths.
6. Promote any named faction quest into its own page once requirements and rewards are stable.

## FAQ

### Can every faction be kept happy?

Possibly for parts of a run, but this page should not claim a perfect route until approval changes and conflicts are confirmed end to end.

### Which faction should I prioritize?

Prioritize the faction tied to your current route goal, then protect enough neutral standing elsewhere to avoid unknown locks.

### Are faction locks confirmed?

Some route effects are likely, but a lock should be treated as pending confirmation until repeated tests show the same option disappearing.

### What is the best way to test factions?

Keep a balanced save as a control, then test one major alignment change at a time and record what changes immediately and later.
