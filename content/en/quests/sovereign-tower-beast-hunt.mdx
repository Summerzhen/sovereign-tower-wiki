## Quick Answer

For Beast Hunt, send a knight who matches the confirmed combat or survival requirement once it is visible in your route. Do not rely on preference alone; check the requirement, check traits, and record success, failure, and unexpected outcome text.

## Beast Hunt Planning Table

| Planning Point | What Players Should Check In-Game |
|---|---|
| Requirement | The exact stat or trait check shown in game |
| Knight fit | Highest relevant stat plus safe trait interaction |
| Equipment | Armor, weapon, or survival gear if the route exposes it |
| Loyalty impact | Whether the assigned knight likes or dislikes hunt quests |
| Outcome | Rewards, injuries, severe failures, or route changes |

## Recommended Steps

1. Open the Beast Hunt quest and write down the visible requirement.
2. Compare available knights by stat match before preference.
3. Check trait text for hunting, beast, danger, or survival interactions.
4. Assign the safest knight and record the result.
5. Rewind only when you can test a different stat, trait, or dialogue branch.

## Assignment Notes

## FAQ

### Who is the best knight for Beast Hunt?

Use the knight whose stats and traits match the visible requirement. A named best pick should be added only after repeated confirmed outcomes.

### Can Beast Hunt injure or kill a knight?

Treat dangerous hunt quests as capable of serious consequences until the failure table is confirmed.

### Should I rewind Beast Hunt?

Rewind if the result reveals a hidden requirement, trait interaction, or avoidable injury.
