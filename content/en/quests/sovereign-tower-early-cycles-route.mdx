## Quick Answer

In the early cycles, scout before you optimize. Use each new quest to learn its visible requirement, damage risk, trait language, and failure behavior, then decide whether to keep the result or rewind for a cleaner clear.

## Early Cycles Overview

| Route Point | What to Do |
|---|---|
| Primary goal | Build reliable route knowledge without avoidable roster damage |
| Stat notes | Treat Wit and Charisma as planning prompts until exact checks are confirmed; log uncertainty separately from stats |
| Good candidates | Compare Angelica, Gwendan, and Dullahan when their visible fit is relevant |
| Best use of rewind | Preserve information from failures, then replay with a better assignment |
| Main risk | Route drift from untracked failures, injuries, loyalty loss, or hidden flags |

## Step-by-Step

1. Before choosing a quest, list the current roster, injuries, loyalty concerns, and unresolved route goals.
2. Open each available quest and copy the exact requirement, reward, warning, and flavor clue.
3. Assign the safest fitting knight when the risk is high; use lower-stakes quests for testing traits.
4. Record success, failure, unexpected outcome text, and any change to roster or quest availability.
5. Use rewind when a result teaches a better assignment or reveals a hidden requirement.
6. Promote repeatable or named early-cycle quests into their own pages once their text and outcomes are confirmed.

## FAQ

### Should I accept every early-cycle failure?

No. Failures are useful when they reveal requirements or consequences, but you should usually rewind if the cost damages a route you care about.

### What stats matter most early?

Wit and Charisma are useful planning categories in the current data, but exact checks must come from the visible quest requirement. Treat uncertainty or swingy results as outcome notes unless the active save shows a stat label.

### When should a quest get its own page?

Create a separate page when the quest name, requirement, risk, and at least one outcome can be described without guesswork.
