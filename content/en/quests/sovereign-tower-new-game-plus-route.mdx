### Is New Game Plus confirmed?

### What should I compare first?

Compare opening state, available knights, visible requirements, and route flags. Those differences matter more than optimizing rewards on the first NG+ test.

It might, but do not assume that. Track recruitment routes, including Brunhilda-related flags, against a normal campaign baseline before making firm claims.
