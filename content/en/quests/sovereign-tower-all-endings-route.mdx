## Quick Answer

The All Endings Route is a tracking page, not a complete ending chart yet. Log faction state, romance choices, major quest outcomes, Dragon Heart clues, and rewind decisions before claiming any ending requirement.

## Ending Ledger

| Column | What to Record |
|---|---|
| Ending name | Exact in-game title or a temporary internal label |
| Required state | Faction, romance, recruitment, route, and system conditions |
| Key quests | Dragon Knight, Factions, Romance Angelica, Full Campaign, and related branches |
| Lockout risk | Choices that may close another ending path |
| Evidence level | Confirmed, reported, route-dependent, or not reproduced |

## Route Process

1. Start with the Full Campaign Route and keep one ledger per campaign pass.
2. Treat ending triggers as route notes until players can repeat them in-game.
3. Compare faction, romance, recruitment, Dragon Heart, and character-route changes.
4. Split a confirmed ending into its own focused page after the trigger is stable.
5. Keep walkthrough advice conservative when branches conflict.
