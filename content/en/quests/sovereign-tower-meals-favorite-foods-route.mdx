## Quick Answer

Use the Meals and Favorite Foods Route as a tracking hub. Record each food event, the assigned knight, visible requirements, and the result before treating a meal as a confirmed favorite food or affinity bonus.

## Tracking Table

| Item | What to check |
|---|---|
| Meal name | Exact in-game wording, not a paraphrased recipe name |
| Knight response | Affinity, loyalty, morale, or only flavor text |
| Requirement | Stat, trait, ingredient, route state, or servant condition |
| Repeatability | Whether the same result appears in the in-game version after replay |
| Risk | Reported bonus values should stay unconfirmed until repeated |

## Route Checklist

1. Log the meal, ingredient prompt, and any servant or kitchen text.
2. Assign by visible requirements first, then compare trait and preference theories.
3. Mark results as confirmed, reported, or not reproduced.
4. Link confirmed events back to the main Meals guide and Cooking Questline.
5. Split named recipes into child pages only after their requirements are stable.
