## Quick Answer

For the Cooking Questline, record every meal, ingredient prompt, kitchen event, servant mention, and affinity change in one place. Do not assume a recipe has a fixed stat gate until the quest UI or repeated outcomes confirm it.

## Cooking Questline Overview

| Route Point | What to Track |
|---|---|
| Scope | Meals, kitchen requests, ingredient prompts, servant support, morale, and affinity clues |
| Stat notes | Treat Charisma and Wit as low-confidence planning prompts; log uncertainty separately from stats |
| Likely fit | Social, gentle, reliable, or servant-facing traits are worth testing when visible text supports them |
| Best candidates | Compare Angelica and Gwendan when the requirement points toward kindness, diplomacy, or utility |
| Main risk | Turning a flavor event into a claimed mechanic before repeated verification |

## Step-by-Step

1. Start a cooking log with columns for event name, ingredients, assigned knight, visible requirement, and result.
2. Copy any servant, kitchen, morale, recovery, or affinity wording exactly enough to compare later.
3. Assign by visible requirement first; only use trait theories when the quest text supports them.
4. Record whether the result changes loyalty, affinity, recovery, resources, or future kitchen options.
5. Re-test any surprising result with a different knight before calling it a confirmed cooking mechanic.
6. Split named recipes or recurring kitchen events into child quest pages once their requirements are stable.

## What to Verify

| Claim to Check | Verification Note |
|---|---|
| Recipe requirements | Confirm whether each event uses stats, traits, ingredients, or route state |
| Servant effects | Track whether kitchen events unlock servants, improve service, or change tower systems |
| Affinity impact | Record exact affinity or loyalty changes rather than assuming meals are always positive |
| Recovery mechanics | Check whether cooking affects injury, fatigue, morale, or only flavor text |
| Repeat events | Separate one-time scenes from repeatable cooking tasks |

## FAQ

### Does cooking improve affinity?

Possibly, but treat it as unconfirmed until a route note shows a clear affinity or loyalty change tied to a cooking event.

### Who should handle cooking quests?

Use the knight who matches the visible requirement. If the text suggests kindness, diplomacy, or servant interaction, Angelica or Gwendan may be useful test candidates when available.

### Should every recipe become its own page?

Only after the recipe name, requirement, and outcome can be documented clearly. Until then, keep recipe notes inside this questline page.
