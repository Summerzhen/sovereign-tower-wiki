## Quick Answer

For the Act 2 Murder Investigation, do not rush to accuse the first suspect. Build a route log that separates evidence, rumor, motive, opportunity, knight testimony, and time-rewind knowledge. Investigation routes are exactly where a wrong assumption can create a convincing but incomplete answer.

Use this page as the investigation hub until every named clue and suspect has a dedicated quest record. For broader routing, start from the [Complete Walkthrough](/guide/sovereign-tower-walkthrough), then cross-check outcomes in the [Quest Database](/quests).

## Investigation Route Checklist

| Step | What to Record |
|---|---|
| Crime scene | Location, cycle, victim, witness, and any first-look clue text |
| Evidence | Physical clue, contradiction, missing item, faction pressure, or testimony |
| Suspect | Motive, opportunity, relationship state, and prior route conflict |
| Knight assignment | Wit, Luck, Charisma, hidden traits, loyalty, and affinity state |
| Accusation | Dialogue choice, proof used, faction reaction, and whether the case closes |
| Rewind test | What changes when you enter the route with prior knowledge |

## Best Stats for Murder Investigation

| Stat | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Wit | Reading contradictions, solving logic chains, and avoiding shallow accusations |
| Luck | Finding hidden or optional clues that may not appear on a normal pass |
| Charisma | Pressing witnesses without creating faction backlash |
| Magic | Checking supernatural explanations only when the route suggests them |

Strength and Agility may still matter if the route branches into pursuit, intimidation, or protection, but they should not be treated as the main investigation stats until the active quest requirement says so.

## Suspect and Evidence Ledger

Use this format when documenting each run. It keeps the page useful without claiming a solved mystery before the route is fully repeatable.

| Suspect / Lead | Evidence For | Evidence Against | Test Needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Record suspect | Record clue or motive | Record contradiction or alibi | Repeat with different knight, choice, or rewind knowledge |
| Record faction lead | Record political pressure | Record missing proof | Compare faction values before and after accusation |
| Record supernatural lead | Record strange clue | Record mundane explanation | Test Magic and Wit assignments separately |

## Rewind Strategy

Investigation routes are good rewind targets because knowledge itself can become a variable. When you rewind, test only one major change at a time:

1. Keep the same suspect and change the knight assignment.
2. Keep the same knight and change one dialogue choice.
3. Keep the same choices and change the order in which you collect clues.
4. Compare a normal accusation against an omniscient or prior-knowledge option.

## FAQ

### Which knight is best for Act 2 Murder Investigation?

Use a knight with strong Wit or Luck first, then check whether loyalty, hidden traits, or Charisma matter in witness scenes. Do not lock in a single best knight until the suspect route is repeatable.

### Can the murder case change later endings?

It may. Treat the accusation result, faction reaction, and any unsolved route state as ending-relevant until full route data proves otherwise.

### Should I rewind after a wrong accusation?

Yes, but write down the failure first. A wrong accusation can reveal which evidence, stat, or route flag was missing.
