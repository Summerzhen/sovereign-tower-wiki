## Quick Answer

For the Alliances route, document every alliance proposal with its source, faction cost, assigned knight, and follow-up effect. Charisma and Wit are useful planning categories, but exact thresholds should stay not yet pinned down until the game exposes them.

## Alliance Overview

| Route Point | Working Plan |
|---|---|
| Primary goal | Secure or reject alliances while preserving enough faction stability for later routes |
| Planning stats | Charisma first, Wit second; log uncertainty separately unless the UI shows a stat label |
| Suggested anchors | Gwendan, Angelica, and Brunhilda are useful comparison picks when available |
| Best traits | Court-facing, diplomatic, and stable-loyalty traits |
| Main risk | One alliance may please an outside power while angering an internal faction |

## Steps / Route Fit

1. Record the audience, quest, or region event that introduces each alliance offer.
2. Note the current standing of Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles, the People, and Treasury before deciding.
3. Assign a knight because the visible text supports their fit, not because a in-game planning table implies a threshold.
4. Compare a diplomatic pick against at least one alternative when rewind allows clean testing.
5. Link alliance results to later Rebellion, Factions, romance, and Full Campaign route notes.
6. Mark an alliance as confirmed only when its support, pressure relief, or follow-up audience repeats.

## What Players Should Check In-Game

| Claim to Check | Player Note |
|---|---|
| Alliance source | Confirm whether the offer comes from a quest, audience petition, region event, or act transition |
| Diplomatic gate | Record exact visible Charisma and Wit requirements where possible |
| Knight image | Test whether loyalty, public reputation, or hidden traits change the negotiation result |
| Faction cost | Track who gains approval and who loses it after the alliance |
| Later consequences | Check whether the alliance changes rebellion pressure, recruitment, romance, or endings |

## FAQ

### Should I accept every alliance?

No. Alliances can be useful, but each one should be judged against faction cost, route priorities, and later political pressure.

### Are Charisma checks confirmed?

Not as exact thresholds on this page. Treat Charisma as the likely diplomatic category until the game displays a requirement or repeated tests confirm it.

### Which knights should be tested first?

Start with court-facing knights such as Gwendan, Angelica, and Brunhilda when available, then compare outcomes against a lower-fit pick.

### Can alliances lock routes?

Possibly, but do not call a lock confirmed until the same alliance prevents the same later option across multiple controlled runs.
