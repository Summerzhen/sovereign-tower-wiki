## Quick Answer

To beat the Dragon Knight in Sovereign Tower, start by checking the quest card in the in-game version, assign knights whose visible Strength, Agility, Magic, Wit, or Charisma match the challenge, and avoid choosing only by preference. Steam's official store page lists those five Knight stats; treat any other value as build-specific until the UI confirms it.

## Recommended Stats

Do not assume the Dragon Knight is only a Strength check. Before assigning the party, review every visible requirement and compare it against each knight's known profile in the in-game version.

| Stat | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Strength | Core combat pressure, duels, physical confrontation |
| Agility | Avoiding damage, positioning, chase or dodge moments |
| Magic | Dragon-linked or supernatural complications |
| Wit | Reading the situation and avoiding bad decisions |

## Best Knight Selection

Pick the knight who can pass the quest first. If a knight dislikes the quest type, you can repair affinity later through dialogue, successful assignments, meals, or relationship choices. A happy but underqualified knight is more dangerous than a mildly annoyed but capable one.

Use this priority order:

1. Match the required stats.
2. Check visible traits.
3. Consider hidden trait information you have already revealed.
4. Confirm armor and equipment are not damaged.
5. Only then consider quest preference and affinity loss.

## Common Mistakes

- Assigning a favorite knight because their affinity is high.
- Treating quest preference as the same thing as quest ability.
- Ignoring hidden traits after an advisor investigation reveals useful information.
- Ending the cycle before comparing damage risk and equipment.
- Rewinding without writing down what the failed assignment taught you.
