## Quick Answer

For Act 0, focus on preserving a clean opening state. Official demo coverage confirms Act 0 as the opening/prologue layer, so record the first audience order, available knights, visible quest requirements, and any choice that the active save shows as persistent.

| Route Point | What to Track |

## Step-by-Step

1. Create a timeline for Act 0 before accepting the first quest or audience decision.
2. List every available knight and any visible stat, trait, loyalty, or preference clue.
3. For each quest, write down the exact requirement shown in the interface.
4. Assign knights by requirement fit first, then record whether preference or trait text changes the outcome.
5. Save notes for tutorial failures separately from permanent route consequences.
6. At the end of Act 0, summarize the roster, unresolved quest leads, and any choices that might affect Act 1.

## FAQ

### Can Act 0 choices block later routes?

Treat that as possible until tested in the active save. Opening choices should be tracked carefully because later recruitment, loyalty, and quest availability may depend on early state.

### Who should I assign in Act 0?

Assign the knight who matches the visible quest requirement. Angelica and Gwendan are useful early comparison candidates when their stats or traits fit, but the UI requirement should decide.

### Is Act 0 the same as the demo?

Not necessarily. Keep Act 0 notes separate from demo notes unless the active save confirms they share the same quests, flags, and progression.
