## Quick Answer

For the Goose Quest, do not judge the assignment only by how silly the quest sounds. Read the in-game quest text, match the visible requirement, and consider traits only when the UI or repeatable outcomes support them.

## Goose Quest Strategy

Treat the Goose Quest as an unusual-outcome candidate until the in-game version confirms its exact requirement. A quest involving a goose may still reward agility, charisma, unusual outcome handling, or a personality trait, but the visible quest card should decide the first assignment.

| Assignment Check | What to Do |
|---|---|
| Quest wording | Look for clues about diplomacy, animals, speed, or danger |
| Knight stats | Match the visible requirement before preference |
| Traits | Animal-friendly or calm traits may matter if revealed |
| Outcome notes | Record whether the result was normal, critical, failed, or unexpected |

## Best Knight Logic

Choose a knight whose stats fit the task. If you know a knight has a trait related to animals, kindness, diplomacy, or unusual outcomes, test that interaction and record the result before using time rewind.
