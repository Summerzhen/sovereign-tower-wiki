## Quick Answer

For Rebellion, prioritize confirmed requirements and loyalty risk before making a dramatic choice. This is the kind of quest where success, failure, and unexpected outcomes may affect later route options, so track every branch.

## Rebellion Planning Table

| Planning Point | What to Track |
|---|---|
| Quest type | Political, combat, intimidation, rescue, or mixed requirement |
| Knight fit | Stats and traits that match the visible requirement |
| Loyalty | Whether the chosen knight supports the ruler's approach |
| Consequence | Rebels defeated, negotiated, empowered, or redirected |

## Recommended Steps

1. Save or prepare a rewind point before accepting Rebellion.
2. Record the visible quest requirement and any warning text.
3. Pick a knight with both stat fit and stable loyalty when possible.
4. Note whether the outcome changes later story text or quest access.
5. Add confirmed branch notes to your route log before moving on.
