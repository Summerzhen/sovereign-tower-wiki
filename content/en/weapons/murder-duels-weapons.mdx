## Why Your Weapon Abilities Make or Break Every Duel

In Murder Duels, every round gives you a knife and a revolver—but the abilities you equip on those weapons determine whether you win or lose. Understanding murder duels weapons and their unique skills is the fastest way to climb the leaderboard and stack up wins. This guide breaks down every ability in the game, ranks the strongest combos, and gives you practical tips for building your loadout.

## How Weapons Work in Murder Duels

Murder Duels is a Roblox deathmatch shooter where players step on a pad to start a duel, and the first player to reach five wins takes the match. Each duel arms you with two weapons—a knife and a revolver—both of which can be customized with unique abilities that change how they function.

Abilities are purchased with in-game coins, and some of the best ones carry steep price tags. Community reports indicate that certain abilities cost up to 20,000 coins, making it important to spend wisely rather than buying everything at random.

You can also unlock and trade weapon skins as you progress, but skins are cosmetic—abilities are what actually shift the outcome of a fight.

Both weapons operate on cooldowns, so timing your ability usage around those cooldowns is critical, especially in team modes like 3v3 and 4v4.

## Every Knife Ability Explained

The knife offers a diverse set of abilities ranging from straightforward damage to clever movement tools. Here is every knife ability currently in the game.

### Triple Knife

Fires three knives in a spread pattern. It is simple and effective for hitting lined-up targets, but player experience suggests it is not the hardest-hitting option. Best used as a reliable, low-risk poke tool.

### Explosive Knife

Launches a projectile that detonates on impact, creating an area-of-effect explosion on the ground. This ability is widely considered one of the strongest knife options because you do not need a direct hit—just landing near your opponent is enough. Community reports consistently call this ability overpowered in both 1v1 and team modes.

### Smoke

Deploys a large smoke cloud on the ground. The smoke dissipates quickly, which limits its usefulness in most situations. Some players speculate that advanced tech or combos may make it viable in the future, but for now it is generally regarded as one of the weaker picks.

### Molotov

Throws a Molotov that creates a fire pool on the ground, dealing 20 damage per tick. Excellent for area denial and chipping down enemies who are forced to walk through choke points. Pairs well with abilities that restrict enemy movement.

### Updraft

Launches you straight into the air. On its own it is a simple vertical boost, but combined with the Portal ability it becomes one of the best movement combos in the game.

### Portal

Throws a projectile that teleports you to its location on activation. The real power of Portal comes from combining it with Updraft—throw the Portal into the air, activate Updraft immediately, and you launch to an extreme height. From there, you can look down and take easy shots at opponents below.

### Decoy

Places a fake player model on the map. If an enemy shoots the decoy, they waste their shot and reveal their position. The decoy disappears quickly, so placement and timing matter. It is a solid ability for baiting shots in 1v1 situations.

## Every Revolver Ability Explained

The revolver abilities focus on enhancing your gunplay with damage modifiers, defensive tools, and information-gathering skills.

### Full Auto Gun

Converts your revolver into a burst-fire weapon that shoots five bullets rapidly. This ability shines at close range where landing multiple shots is easy, but it struggles at distance because the spread makes it hard to connect. Save this for tight corridors and close-quarters duels.

### Ricochet Bullet

Fires a bullet that bounces off walls and surfaces. In team modes, the ricochet can chain between multiple players, making it absurdly strong in 4v4 matches. Player experience confirms that a single Ricochet Bullet can damage or eliminate two opponents at once when aimed near walls and corners.

### Quick Reload

Grants an instant reload, letting you fire another shot immediately. This is valuable in team fights where securing a quick kill can shift a round in your favor. However, community reports note that it is quite expensive for what it does, so consider whether your coin budget is better spent elsewhere first.

### Icy Bullets

Freezes your gun, reducing body shot damage to 75 but slowing enemies on hit. The trade-off is significant—you sacrifice raw damage for crowd control. In 4v4 modes, slowing an enemy so your teammates can finish them off has value, but in 1v1 duels most players prefer killing outright rather than slowing.

### Deflect

Activates a defensive stance. If an enemy hits you while Deflect is active, they become stunned and you take no damage. This effectively gives you a free kill if timed correctly—bait the enemy shot, deflect it, and then punish their stun. Widely considered one of the best revolver abilities in the game.

### Sprint

Gives you a burst of movement speed. Straightforward and universally useful for repositioning, chasing down kills, or escaping bad situations. A solid pick on any map.

### Wall Hack

Lets you see enemy positions through walls for a duration. This is an incredibly strong information tool that lets you pre-aim corners and catch opponents off guard. Player experience ranks this as one of the most consistently useful abilities, especially on maps with lots of cover.

## Best Ability Combos for Winning Duels

Individual abilities are strong, but combining your knife and revolver abilities into synergistic loadouts is where Murder Duels weapons truly shine. Below are the top combos based on community gameplay.

### Portal + Updraft (Mobility/Dominance)

Throw the Portal high into the air, activate Updraft as you teleport, and you launch to massive height. From above, you have a clear line of sight on opponents while they struggle to hit you. This combo is exceptional on open maps where aerial positioning gives you a huge advantage.

### Ricochet Bullet + Wall Hack (Information/Damage)

Use Wall Hack to locate enemies behind cover, then fire Ricochet Bullets off walls to hit them without exposing yourself. This combo is devastating in team modes where enemies tend to group up near cover.

### Explosive Knife + Ricochet Bullet (Area Pressure)

The Explosive Knife forces enemies out of position with its blast radius, and the Ricochet Bullet punishes them as they reposition. This combo works on almost every map and is beginner-friendly because neither ability requires pinpoint accuracy.

### Molotov + Ricochet Bullet (Zone Control)

Throw the Molotov to deny an area, then use Ricochet Bullets to chip away at enemies who are forced into predictable paths. The Molotov deals consistent tick damage while the ricochet shots finish them off.

## Tips for Choosing and Using Your Abilities

Picking the right abilities is only half the battle. How you use them matters just as much. Keep these principles in mind:

- **Match your abilities to the mode.** Ricochet Bullet and Molotov excel in team modes where enemies cluster. Deflect and Decoy are stronger in 1v1 duels where baiting a single shot can win the round.
- **Respect cooldowns.** Every ability has a cooldown. Using your Deflect or Portal at the wrong time leaves you vulnerable. Track your cooldowns and save key abilities for critical moments.
- **Prioritize high-impact abilities first.** If you are on a budget, buy Deflect, Explosive Knife, and Ricochet Bullet before anything else. These three give you the most value per coin spent.
- **Practice combos in the range.** Murder Duels includes a practice range where you can test abilities before taking them into matches. Spend time there learning Portal placement and Ricochet angles.
- **Adapt to the map.** Tight maps favor Full Auto Gun and Molotov. Open maps favor Portal + Updraft and Wall Hack. Adjust your loadout based on where you are playing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best murder duels weapons abilities for beginners?

Deflect and Explosive Knife are the two best starting abilities. Deflect rewards smart timing rather than raw aim, and Explosive Knife deals damage without requiring direct hits. Add Ricochet Bullet next for strong team-mode performance.

### How do I unlock abilities in Murder Duels?

Abilities are purchased with in-game coins earned from winning duels. Some abilities cost as much as 20,000 coins according to community reports, so focus on high-impact picks first rather than buying everything.

### Can I use abilities in team modes like 4v4?

Yes. Abilities work in all duel formats. In fact, some abilities like Ricochet Bullet and Icy Bullets are significantly stronger in team modes because they affect multiple opponents or support teammates.

### Is the Portal + Updraft combo hard to use?

It takes practice. You need to throw the Portal into the air and activate Updraft at the right moment during the teleport. Spend time in the practice range getting the timing down before using it in ranked matches. Once you master it, the combo gives you unmatched vertical positioning.
