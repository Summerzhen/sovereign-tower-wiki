## Why Guns Matter in Murder Duels

Murder Duels blends the tension of Murder Mystery with the fast-paced arena combat of Roblox Rivals, and your gun is your most reliable tool for securing kills. Whether you're dueling in a 1v1 or fighting in a 3v3, understanding how murder duels guns work—from the default revolver to legendary options—gives you a serious edge over opponents who just point and shoot.

This guide breaks down every confirmed gun mechanic, damage behavior, and skill interaction so you can stop guessing and start winning.

## The Default Revolver: Your Core Weapon

Every player in Murder Duels spawns with the same loadout: a revolver and a knife. There are no class selections or weapon pickups on the map. The revolver is your primary ranged option, and mastering it is non-negotiable.

### How the Revolver Handles

Based on player experience, the revolver functions as a semi-automatic sidearm. You fire individual shots and must reload after emptying your magazine. Key handling details include:

- **Aiming down sights** is possible, but community reports suggest the aim-in mechanic feels clunky and may not improve accuracy enough to justify the slower movement.
- **Hip-firing** is generally preferred by experienced players for maintaining mobility during duels.
- **Hit markers** appear on successful shots, confirming your damage.

### The Reload Trick Every Player Should Know

One of the most important revolver mechanics is the **switch-reload trick**. When you fire your revolver, you can immediately switch to your knife and throw it—by the time you switch back, the revolver has reloaded automatically. This means you never have to stand still waiting for a reload animation.

This technique effectively doubles your damage output in close-range fights:

| Step | Action | Result |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Fire revolver shot | Deal damage, trigger reload |
| 2 | Switch to knife | Revolver continues reloading in background |
| 3 | Throw knife | Deal additional damage |
| 4 | Switch back to revolver | Weapon is reloaded and ready |

This combo—revolver shot into knife throw—is described by players as one of the cleanest and most effective attack sequences in the game.

## Damage Numbers and Range Mechanics

Understanding why your shots deal different amounts of damage is crucial for improving your gunplay. During gameplay, players have reported inconsistent damage numbers that suggest a range-based damage system.

### Observed Damage Values

| Damage Number | Possible Cause | Notes |
|---------------|----------------|-------|
| 75 | Long-range hit | Player reports suggest distance reduces damage |
| 39 | Extreme range or glancing hit | Described as feeling underwhelming |
| Full damage (unspecified) | Close to mid-range | Standard kill potential |

Community reports indicate that landing hits at longer distances results in reduced damage—commonly showing as 75 rather than a full-damage shot. This means positioning matters: closing the gap before firing often yields better results than sniping from across the map.

### Practical Takeaway

If you're consistently seeing lower damage numbers, try closing distance before engaging. The revolver rewards aggressive, mid-range play more than passive long-range poking.

## Skills That Enhance Your Gun Play

Murder Duels features a skill system that lets you customize your loadout before matches. In modes like 3v3, you can equip two skills simultaneously. Several skills directly or indirectly improve your effectiveness with murder duels guns.

### Decoy: The Overpowered Pick

Multiple players have called the Decoy skill borderline overpowered. When deployed, it creates a clone that stands in place until an enemy destroys it. This serves two purposes:

- **Distraction**: Enemies waste time and ammo shooting the decoy.
- **Bait**: You can position your decoy to lure opponents into your line of fire.

For gun-focused players, the Decoy is arguably the strongest skill because it creates predictable engagement windows where you can land full-damage revolver shots.

### X-Ray: Information Is Power

The X-Ray skill reveals enemy positions through walls. While the exact duration and cooldown aren't officially documented, the tactical advantage is clear: you can pre-aim around corners and land first-shot hits before your opponent even sees you.

## Combat Strategies for Gun Players

### Don't Aim Down Sights

This might sound counterintuitive, but player feedback consistently indicates that aiming down sights in Murder Duels feels sluggish and unrewarding. Hip-firing keeps you mobile and harder to hit while maintaining solid accuracy at the revolver's effective range.

### Use the Knife as a Reload Tool

Treat your knife not just as a backup weapon but as an integral part of your gun combo. The switch-reload trick ensures you're always dealing damage instead of waiting. Remember: you must **press and hold** to throw the knife—a quick tap won't work.

### Control Your Range

Since damage drops off at distance, avoid long-range gunfights. Use map geometry—breaking through glass, bouncing off surfaces, or cutting through interior spaces—to close the gap before engaging with your revolver.

### Leverage Water Bounces on Yacht

On the Yacht map, you can bounce off the water once. A second bounce kills you. Use this to perform unexpected aerial angles that confuse opponents and give you brief high-ground shots with your revolver.

### Skill Combinations for Different Modes

| 2v2 | Decoy + Explosive Knife | Area control plus distraction |
| 3v3 | Decoy + Explosive Knife | Splash damage shines with more targets; decoy scales with player count |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Tunnel-visioning on trick shots**: Going for 360 knife kills looks cool but often costs you the round. Prioritize consistent revolver shots over flashy plays.
- **Ignoring damage falloff**: If you're hitting 75s or 39s, you're too far away. Reposition before continuing the fight.
- **Wasting the decoy**: Don't throw your decoy in obvious spots. Place it where enemies will naturally look or path toward.
- **Forgetting knife hold mechanic**: Tapping the knife button does nothing. You must hold to throw.
- **Static positioning**: Murder Duels maps have exploitable geometry. Standing still makes you an easy target for knife throws and coordinated pushes.
