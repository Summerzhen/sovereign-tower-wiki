## Best Quest Fit

Goberto should be tested first on hunt, duel, and physical assignments where Strength matters. Before sending him to major route quests, compare the visible stat requirements in the [Quest Finder](/quests) and check whether low Agility, damaged armor, or hidden trait risk could turn the result bad.

## Dullahan Branch Warning

Multiple public recruitment sources connect Goberto's death to the Dullahan route. Do not treat that as a casual experiment on a main save: record the quest, armor, cause of death, and whether Dullahan appears afterward.

## FAQ

### Is Goberto a starter knight?

Current public recruitment data places him in the early roster around Day 2 / Cycle 2 rather than as the very first knight. Verify the exact timing in your active build.

### Should I let Goberto die to get Dullahan?

Only if you are intentionally testing that branch. His death can affect roster strength, route state, and later assignments.
