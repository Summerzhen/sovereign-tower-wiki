## Quick Answer

Sovereign Tower knights should be evaluated by stats and traits first, then by affinity and quest preference. A knight can dislike a quest and still be the best person for it; preference mainly affects satisfaction, while visible requirement matching affects the result.

## Knight Database Fields

Use this page as the parent hub for individual knight profiles. Each knight should eventually have a structured page with the same fields so quests can link directly to their best assignments.

| Field | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Stats | Determines quest fit |
| Traits | Can improve, block, or change outcomes |
| Hidden traits | Rewards investigation and repeated cycles |
| Preferences | Affects affinity and satisfaction |
| Best quests | Creates direct links from knight to quest pages |
| Recruitment | Captures audience and route requirements from the current build |

## Known Knight Strategy

The safest rule is simple: do not confuse liking a quest with being good at a quest. If a knight has the right Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, or Wit, they may still be the correct choice even when the assignment costs a little affinity.
