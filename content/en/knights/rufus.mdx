## FAQ

### Is Rufus a good early pick?

He can be valuable if your route has already moved The Wolf toward the curse-breaking path. Do not plan around Rufus in a route that killed the beast or recruited Silgur instead.

### Can I recruit Rufus and Silgur together?

Do not assume that in a normal route. Public guides frame Rufus as the curse path and Silgur as the kill-the-beast-without-Angelica path, so test exclusivity before promising both.

### Should Rufus handle diplomacy?

Probably not as a first test. Use him for physical routes unless the game displays a trait or item that changes his social fit.
