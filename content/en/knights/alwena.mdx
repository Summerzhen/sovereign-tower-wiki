## Quick Answer

Alwena is reported as an emergency recruit who can volunteer when you open the Round Table with no available knights in Act 2 or later. Because this is a one-time style condition, record the roster state before testing it.

## FAQ

### Can Alwena resign?

Current public all-knights data reports that she does not resign, but this should still be verified in-game before being used as hard route advice.

### Is Alwena good for long quests?

Public data describes an Intendant limitation around quests lasting more than one cycle. Check the active quest duration before assigning her.
