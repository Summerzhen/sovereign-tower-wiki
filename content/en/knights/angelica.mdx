## FAQ

### Are Angelica's stats confirmed?

Treat them as in-game values to recheck before publishing exact numbers. Angelica is likely the easiest Knight to check because she appears early and can be screenshotted cleanly.

### Why is Angelica tied to meals?

Meals are one of the best places to test affinity and preference behavior. Angelica gives the site a stable early-game page for recording those reactions without guessing.

### What should complete this page later?

Confirmed stat screenshots, exact recruitment context, hidden trait trigger, favorite foods, disliked foods, and at least two quest outcomes that show where Angelica performs well.
