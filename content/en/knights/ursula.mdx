## FAQ

### Is Ursula bad?

No. The caution is about fit. She may be valuable when the assignment asks for force, magic, resolve, or direct action, but she is a poor first guess for social jobs unless the quest text shows a twist.

### Should Ursula handle Charisma quests?

Avoid that until a confirmed trait, item, or route condition says otherwise. Existing coverage warns against using her as a charm pick.

### Is Ursula tied to betrayal routes?

There are route reports worth tracking, but this page should not present betrayal or late-route details as settled without controlled evidence.
