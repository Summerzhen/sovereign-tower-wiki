## What Is Sovereign Tower?

Sovereign Tower is a story-rich, Round Table management RPG developed by Wild Wits Games and published by Curve Games. In it, you play as the Sovereign of a magical tower, recruiting eccentric Knights, assigning them to quests, and balancing the egos and drama of your court while shaping your kingdom's destiny.

### Core Gameplay Pillars

The game blends several genres into a unique experience:

- **Choices Matter**: Every decision you make shapes your kingdom, your relationships, and your legacy
- **Knight Management**: Recruit Knights with strong personalities and questionable morals, then assign them to quests based on their stats
- **Time Manipulation**: A Demon in the Crypts lets you rewind time, retry decisions, and uncover hidden secrets
- **Kingdom Balancing**: Manage affinity with Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles, and the People while keeping the Treasury healthy
- **Tower Expansion**: Build new annexes like Carina's Forge or the Witch's Alchemy Room to craft items for your Knights

Each Knight has five core stats—Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, and Wit—and each quest requires a different skill set. Send the wrong Knight and you might face a Critical Failure. Send the right one and you could see a Critical Success or even an Unexpected Outcome.

### Why Console Players Want It

The game's hand-drawn art style, dialogue-heavy narrative, and point-and-click mechanics translate well to a controller. The Steam version already lists PlayStation controller support in its accessibility features, which is a promising sign for anyone tracking the Sovereign Tower PS5 release date.
