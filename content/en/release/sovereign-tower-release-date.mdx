## A Day in the Life of a Sovereign

The game structures your time around a daily routine that drives all of its systems. Understanding this loop is key to enjoying what Sovereign Tower offers.

**Morning �?Tend to Your Subjects**

Each day begins with petitions from your people. Problems range from mundane tasks like gardening to more dramatic requests such as exorcisms. The requests you choose to address �?and how you address them �?ripple outward into your faction relationships and the options available to you later.

**Midday �?Manage the Round Table**

After hearing from your subjects, you assign Knights to handle the requests you've accepted. This is where the management layer shines. Each Knight has distinct stats across five categories:

| Stat | Best For |
|------|----------|
| Strength | Physical challenges, combat-related quests |
| Agility | Tasks requiring speed or finesse |
| Charisma | Diplomatic missions, social encounters |
| Magic | Supernatural and mystical quests |
| Wit | Puzzle-solving, strategic assignments |

Matching the right Knight to the right quest significantly impacts the outcome. Send the wrong person and you might face a Critical Failure. Send the right one and you could see a Critical Success �?or something entirely unexpected.

**Evening �?Debrief and Decide**

When Knights return from their quests, you'll receive a report on what happened. Outcomes aren't always binary. The game features Critical Failures, Critical Successes, and Unexpected Outcomes that can open new story branches or create problems you didn't anticipate.

**When Things Go Wrong �?Turn Back Time**

The Demon in the Crypts allows you to rewind time with full knowledge of what happened. This isn't just a retry mechanic �?it's a core narrative device. You can make different choices, pursue alternate paths, uncover secrets you missed, and even explore forbidden romances that might have been locked off on your first attempt.
