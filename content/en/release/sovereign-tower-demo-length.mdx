## What Affects Your Demo Playtime

Several mechanics and design choices in Sovereign Tower directly influence how long your demo session lasts. Understanding these helps you plan your time and decide how deeply you want to explore.

### Dialogue Choices and Branching Paths

Every decision you make shapes your kingdom's relationships with individuals, factions, and other rulers. The game tracks your affinity with five groups: Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles, and the People, plus your Treasury. Different choices lead to different outcomes, which means a single Act I playthrough only shows you one version of events.

### The Demon Time-Rewind Mechanic

This is the biggest playtime multiplier. The Demon in the Crypts of the Tower lets you rewind time with knowledge you've already gained. You can:

- Undo poor decisions and try alternate approaches
- Unlock new dialogue paths that were previously inaccessible
- Uncover hidden characters you missed on your first pass
- Pursue different romance options (yes, including romancing a stone statue — the Lady of the Tower)

Each rewind essentially gives you a partial new playthrough without starting from absolute zero. If you're the type of player who needs to see every outcome, this feature alone can double or triple your demo time.

### Knight Management Depth

Your Knights have five stats — Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, and Wit — and each quest requires a different skill set. Assigning the wrong Knight can lead to Critical Failure or Unexpected Outcomes. Experimenting with different Knight-quest combinations to find optimal results adds strategic playtime.

### Quest Outcome Variability

Quests can result in Critical Success, Critical Failure, or Unexpected Outcomes. Seeing all possible results for even a handful of quests requires multiple attempts, especially since the demo includes the time-rewind feature to facilitate exactly this kind of experimentation.

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of the Demo

If you want to maximize your Sovereign Tower demo experience without wasting time, here are some practical recommendations:

1. **Play your first run without rewinding.** Experience Act I naturally to understand the story and mechanics before you start optimizing.
2. **Pay attention to Knight stats.** Before assigning quests, check whether Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, or Wit is needed. Sending the wrong Knight wastes a quest slot.
3. **Balance your faction affinity early.** Neglecting one group can lock you out of certain options later. Try to keep relationships relatively even in your first pass.
4. **Use the Intendant for gossip.** She collects secrets about your Knights. This information can help you make better management decisions.
5. **Explore the annexes.** The Blacksmith and Witch can craft items that improve Knight performance on quests. Don't ignore these upgrades.
6. **Save before major decisions.** The game allows saving anytime. Even though you have the Demon rewind, manual saves give you more control.
7. **Talk to the Lady of the Tower.** Without spoiling anything — just do it. The demo hints at romance options that are worth discovering.
