## What You Actually Get for the Sovereign Tower Price

Understanding the price is only half the equation — knowing what the game delivers helps you decide if it's worth the cost. Here's a quick breakdown of the core experience:

- **Story-rich management RPG.** You play as the Sovereign of a magical tower, making decisions that shape your kingdom's destiny.
- **Knight recruitment and Round Table management.** Recruit eccentric heroes, assign them to quests based on their stats (Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, Wit), and deal with their drama.
- **Time manipulation.** A Demon in the Crypts lets you rewind decisions, uncover secrets, and pursue alternate story paths — including forbidden romances.
- **Kingdom balancing.** Manage relationships with Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles, and the People while keeping the Treasury healthy.
- **Tower expansion.** Build annexes like Carina's Forge and the Witch's Alchemy Room to craft items that help your Knights succeed on quests.
- **75 Steam Achievements** to chase across multiple playthroughs.

The game supports six languages for interface and subtitles (English, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese), is Steam Deck Verified, and includes accessibility features like save-anytime, custom volume controls, and no quick-time events.
