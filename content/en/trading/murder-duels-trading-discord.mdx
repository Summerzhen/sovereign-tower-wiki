## How to Trade Safely in MVSD

Scams are the biggest threat in any Roblox trading community. Using a murder duels trading Discord with verified middlemen dramatically reduces your risk, but you still need to follow best practices.

### Always Use a Middleman

A middleman (often abbreviated as MM) is a trusted third party who holds both traders' items and distributes them only when both sides have fulfilled their agreement. Never skip this step for high-value trades. Servers like Rgc's Universe and Middleman Hub exist specifically because of how common direct-trade scams are.

### Verify Your Middleman

Not every middleman is legitimate. Before handing over any items:

- Check the server's verified MM list
- Look for vouches and reviews from other players
- Confirm the middleman's identity through the server's official channels
- Never accept a middleman suggested by the other trader alone

### Understand Item Values Before Trading

Values in MVSD shift based on demand, rarity, and updates. Using a resource like CoreValues' website can help you check whether a proposed trade is in your favor. Cross-referencing multiple value lists is always smarter than relying on a single source.

### Red Flags to Watch For

- A trader refuses to use a middleman
- Someone pressures you to complete a trade quickly
- Offers that seem too good to be true usually are
- Direct messages from people claiming to be staff without verification

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of MVSD Trading Discords

If you're new to MVSD trading, begin with lower-value trades to learn how the process works. Watch how experienced traders negotiate and how middlemen facilitate transactions before committing your best items.

Many servers have vouch systems or feedback channels. Completing successful trades and following server rules builds your reputation, which makes other traders more willing to deal with you—especially for high-value items.

### Stay Active in Value Discussions

Item values in MVSD aren't static. Participating in value discussions on servers like CoreValues helps you stay informed about current market trends and avoid making trades you'll regret.

### Take Advantage of Giveaways

Most active servers run regular giveaways for items, Robux, or even Discord Nitro. While you shouldn't rely on giveaways for progression, they're a nice bonus that can supplement your trading inventory.

### Know the Rules

Every server has its own rules about advertising, cross-trading, and conduct. Read the rules channel carefully before posting. Violating rules can get you banned, cutting off access to that community's trading network.
