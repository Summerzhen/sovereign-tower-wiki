## Why Murder Duels Trading Matters

Trading is one of the most exciting features in Murder Duels, letting players swap weapons and cosmetics they no longer need for items they actually want. If you have duplicate knives or guns sitting in your inventory, the Murder Duels trading system gives you a way to turn those extras into something valuable. Introduced in Update 7, this feature completely changed how players build their collections and interact with each other in the game.

Whether you are chasing the legendary Megalodon Gun or just trying to complete your set of kill effects, understanding how trades work will save you time and help you avoid bad deals. This guide covers the core mechanics, what you can trade, and practical strategies for getting the most out of every exchange.

## How the Trading System Works

The trading system in Murder Duels allows two players to propose and accept item exchanges through an in-game interface. When both players agree on the terms, the items swap ownership instantly. The system was officially released as part of Update 7, marking a major milestone for the game's social and economic features.

### Initiating a Trade

To start a trade, you typically approach another player and use the trade request option. Once the other player accepts, a trade window opens where both parties can add items from their inventories. Each side reviews what is being offered before confirming the deal.

### What You Can Trade

According to the official Murder Duels Wiki, the trading system supports several item categories. Here is a breakdown of every tradable item type:

| Item Category | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Guns | Megalodon Gun, standard firearms | Weapon skins and models |
| Knives | Megalodon Knife, basic blades | Melee cosmetic options |
| Cases | Unopened cases | Contents remain a mystery until opened |
| Kill Effects | Various effect animations | Change how eliminations look |
| Trading Tokens (T$) | Trade Token currency | Used as trade balancing currency |

The inclusion of Trading Tokens, displayed as T$ in the game, is especially important. Tokens act as a flexible currency that players can add to either side of a trade to even out differences in value. If one player is offering a legendary item and the other is offering a rare one, T$ can bridge the gap.

### Items Outside the Trade Window

Based on community reports and player experience, certain items may not be eligible for trading. Skills like X-Ray and Decoy, for example, appear in the game's navigation but are not listed as tradable categories on the official wiki. If you cannot place an item into the trade window, it likely falls into a non-tradable category.

## Trading Tokens (T$) Explained

Trading Tokens serve as the backbone of the Murder Duels trading economy. When item-for-item trades do not line up evenly, T$ provides a way to make both sides feel satisfied with the deal.

### How Tokens Function in a Trade

When you open a trade window, you can add T$ from your balance just like you would add a gun or knife. The other player sees the token amount on your side of the offer and can decide whether the total package is acceptable. Tokens are consumed once the trade completes.

### Earning Trading Tokens

While the wiki does not yet detail all methods of obtaining T$, player experience suggests the following common sources:

- Completing in-game challenges or objectives
- Participating in special events
- Redeeming active codes during trading update promotions
- Receiving tokens as part of previous trades

Always check for new codes when a trading update drops, as developers often release limited-time rewards that include T$ or exclusive items.

## Tips for Safe and Fair Trading

Trading in Murder Duels is generally straightforward, but a few smart habits will protect you from regret and potential scams.

### Before You Trade Checklist

Go through this checklist every time you consider a trade:

- [ ] Verify the exact items on both sides of the trade window
- [ ] Confirm the item rarity matches your expectations
- [ ] Check that the T$ amount, if included, is correct
- [ ] Never feel pressured to accept a trade on the spot
- [ ] Compare the offer to what you have seen in other trades
- [ ] Remember that confirmed trades cannot be reversed

### Common Trading Mistakes to Avoid

- **Rushing the confirmation**: Always double-check the final trade window before clicking accept. A swapped item or changed token amount can turn a good deal into a bad one.
- **Overvaluing cases**: Unopened cases carry risk because you do not know their contents. A case might contain something great or something common, so trade them cautiously.
- **Undervaluing legendary items**: The Megalodon Gun and Megalodon Knife are top-tier. Do not let another player convince you that a handful of common items equals one legendary.
- **Ignoring token balances**: T$ has real utility. If a trade seems slightly in your favor but the other player adds a small token amount, the deal may actually be balanced or even against you.

### Fair Trade Evaluation Framework

When you receive a trade offer, ask yourself these questions:

1. Would I be happy with what I am receiving even if I could never trade it again?
2. Is the rarity of what I am giving up roughly equal to what I am getting?
3. Does the T$ amount compensate for any gap in value?
4. Have I seen similar trades accepted by other players?

If the answer to all four is yes, the trade is likely fair.

## Building Your Collection Through Trading

Trading is not just about individual deals—it is a long-term strategy for completing your Murder Duels inventory.

### Setting Collection Goals

Decide early what you want to focus on. Some players prioritize owning every legendary item, while others collect specific weapon types or kill effects. Having clear goals prevents impulse trades that leave you with items you do not actually want.

### Trading Up Strategy

One effective approach is to trade multiple lower-value items for a single higher-value one. For example, trading several standard knives plus some T$ for a legendary Megalodon Knife can accelerate your collection progress. The key is patience—wait for the right partner who values what you are offering.

### Diversifying Your Inventory

Holding a variety of item types makes you a more attractive trading partner. If you only have guns, your potential trade partners are limited. Keeping a mix of guns, knives, cases, and kill effects gives you more flexibility to negotiate.

### Trade Inventory Management Table

| Strategy | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Trade Up | Exchange multiple lower items for one high-value item | Players chasing legendaries |
| Trade Down | Swap one valuable item for several you need | Players filling gaps in collections |
| Token Focus | Prioritize earning and saving T$ | Players who want maximum flexibility |
| Specialize | Collect only one item type deeply | Players with a clear aesthetic goal |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What items can I trade in Murder Duels?

The Murder Duels trading system allows players to exchange Guns, Knives, Cases, Kill Effects, and Trading Tokens (T$). These five categories cover the main tradable items in the game. Skills like X-Ray and Decoy are not currently listed as tradable according to the official wiki.

### When was trading added to Murder Duels?

Trading was released in Update 7. This update introduced the full trading system, enabling players to swap items and tokens directly with each other rather than relying solely on drops or code redemptions.

### How do Trading Tokens work?

Trading Tokens, shown as T$ in the game, function as a currency you can add to either side of a trade. They help balance deals where the items being exchanged are not equal in perceived value. Once a trade is confirmed, the tokens transfer to the other player and are deducted from your balance.

### Can I reverse a completed trade in Murder Duels trading?

No. Based on player experience and standard Roblox trading mechanics, once both players confirm a trade, the exchange is final. There is no built-in undo button. This is why it is critical to review every detail in the trade window before accepting any offer.
