## Understanding MVSD Item Values

Trading without understanding item values is a quick way to lose out. In Murderers vs Sheriffs Duels, item worth is driven by several factors that shift over time.

### What Affects Item Value

- **Rarity**: Items that drop less frequently or were available for a limited time tend to hold higher value.
- **Demand**: Even a rare item may trade poorly if nobody wants it. Popular weapon skins or effects command premium prices.
- **Supply Changes**: If a code or event re-releases an item, its value typically drops.
- **Community Consensus**: Values in MVSD are largely community-driven. Discord servers like CoreValues serve as the meeting ground where these values are debated and established.

### How to Stay Updated

Values in MVSD are not set by the developers. They are determined by what players are willing to accept. This means values can shift quickly. To stay current:

- Check value discussion channels daily
- Watch completed trades to see what items actually sell for, not just what people ask
- Ask questions if you are unsure about a trade offer
- Be skeptical of deals that seem too good to be true

### Common Trading Mistakes

- Accepting the first offer you receive without checking values
- Trading based on outdated price lists
- Letting pressure from other traders rush your decision
- Not screenshotting trade agreements as proof if issues arise

## Staying Safe While Trading

Even in well-moderated servers, you need to protect yourself. Here are essential safety practices for any MVSD trader.

### Verify Before You Trade

- Confirm the other player's Roblox username matches their Discord identity
- Check if the server has a verified trader role or feedback system
- Ask for references from previous trades if dealing with high-value items

### Document Everything

- Screenshot trade agreements in Discord before completing in-game trades
- Keep records of completed trades for your own reference
- Report any suspicious behavior to server moderators immediately

### Trust Your Instincts

If a deal feels wrong, walk away. No trade is worth losing your items to a scam. The MVSD trading community has plenty of honest players, so there is no reason to rush into a questionable deal.

### Avoid Common Scam Tactics

- Someone offering significantly more than your item is worth
- Pressure to trade quickly before you can check values
- Requests to trade outside the normal in-game system
- Players refusing to use middlemen for high-value trades when the server offers that service
