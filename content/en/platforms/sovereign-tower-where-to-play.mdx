## What to Expect From Sovereign Tower

Before choosing your platform, it helps to understand what kind of experience you're signing up for. Sovereign Tower blends several genres—visual novel, interactive fiction, role-playing, and simulation—into a management RPG where your decisions carry real weight.

### Core Gameplay Pillars

- **Kingdom Management**: Each morning, you tend to your subjects' problems, from mundane gardening requests to supernatural exorcisms. Every decision shapes your relationships and your kingdom's future.
- **Round Table Knight Management**: Recruit Knights with distinct personalities and stats (Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, and Wit), then assign them to quests that match their skill sets.
- **Affinity Balancing**: Maintain—or manipulate—your standing with six factions: Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles, the People, and the Treasury.
- **Time Rewind Mechanic**: A Demon in the Tower's Crypts allows you to turn back time, retaining knowledge from previous attempts to make different choices and alter outcomes.

### Quest Outcomes and Knight Dynamics

When your Knights return from assigned quests, the results fall into three categories:

| Outcome Type | What It Means |
|-------------|---------------|
| Critical Success | The Knight excelled; maximum rewards and positive story consequences |
| Critical Failure | The Knight failed badly; potential negative repercussions for your kingdom |
| Unexpected Outcome | A surprise twist that introduces new story elements or complications |

Matching the right Knight to the right quest—based on their stats—is crucial. Sending a low-Charisma Knight on a diplomatic mission, for example, could lead to disaster. The game's advisor, Arlin, even hints at this with humorous warnings about certain Knights being poor fits for specific tasks.

### Tower Expansion and Crafting

As you progress, you can expand your Tower by building new Annexes. Each Annex unlocks new crafting possibilities and court positions:

- **Blacksmith's Forge**: Craft equipment to boost Knight performance on quests
- **Witch's Alchemy Room**: Brew potions and magical aids
- Additional Annexes open more positions in your court, expanding your strategic options

### Secrets, Romance, and Drama

Sovereign Tower doesn't shy away from interpersonal drama. Your Knights come with strong personalities and questionable morals, leading to advice-seeking, grievances, and even romance options. The Intendant serves as your gossip source, collecting secrets about your Knights that can inform your decisions. And yes, if you're interested in romancing a stone statue, the Lady of the Tower awaits.

## Tips for Getting Started on Either Platform

No matter which version you choose, these tips will help you settle into your role as Sovereign more smoothly.

### Before Your First Session

- **Bookmark the itch.io page** for easy access and update checking
- **Read the development log**—the "Welcome to the tower, Sovereign" post from January 2026 provides helpful context

### During Your First Playthrough

- **Pay attention to Knight stats**: Each quest has different skill requirements, and mismatched assignments lead to failure
- **Don't neglect faction affinity**: Balancing relationships with the Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles, the People, and the Treasury keeps your kingdom stable
- **Use the time rewind freely**: The Demon in the Crypts exists for a reason—if a decision goes poorly, go back and try a different path with the knowledge you've gained

### Managing Multiple Runs

The time-rewind mechanic encourages experimentation, but if you want to start a completely fresh playthrough:

- **HTML5 players**: Clear your browser's local storage for the itch.io domain, or use an incognito/private window
- **Windows players**: Delete or rename your save folder before launching the game again
