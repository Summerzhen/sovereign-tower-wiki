## What Switch Players Can Expect From Sovereign Tower

Whether you're waiting for a potential Switch release or planning to play on PC, here's a breakdown of what Sovereign Tower actually offers. Understanding the gameplay loop will help you decide if this is a title worth keeping on your wishlist.

### Daily Ruler Responsibilities

Each day as Sovereign follows a structured rhythm. In the morning, you tend to your subjects and hear their problems — which range from mundane gardening requests to full-blown exorcisms. Every decision you make shapes your kingdom and your relationships with individuals, factions, and rival rulers.

You'll need to balance your affinity across six key factions:

- **Merchants** — Trade and commerce interests
- **Mystics** — Magical and spiritual concerns
- **Scholars** — Knowledge and research priorities
- **Nobles** — Aristocratic and political matters
- **The People** — Common folk and their daily struggles
- **The Treasury** — Financial health of the kingdom

You can choose to maintain equilibrium among these groups, or go rogue and backstab everyone. The choice is yours, and each path carries consequences.

### Round Table Management

Your Knights are the backbone of your kingdom, but they come with outsized personalities and questionable morals. Managing them is as much about diplomacy as it is about strategy.

Each Knight has five core stats:

| Stat | Use Case |
|------|----------|
| Strength | Physical confrontations and labor-intensive quests |
| Agility | Tasks requiring speed or stealth |
| Charisma | Diplomatic and social encounters |
| Magic | Supernatural and mystical challenges |
| Wit | Puzzle-solving and intellectual quests |

Matching the right Knight to the right quest reduces the chance of failure. Send the wrong person and you risk a Critical Failure or a completely Unexpected Outcome — which can be entertaining but costly.

### Knight Satisfaction and Court Drama

Your Knights aren't silent tools. They have grievances, advice, and even romantic interests. If you fail to keep them satisfied, they may leave your court permanently. Sometimes that's a relief, but losing a skilled Knight can hurt your kingdom's capabilities.

Key interactions with your Knights include:

- Taking their advice on kingdom matters
- Addressing their personal grievances
- Exploring romance options with certain characters
- Managing rivalries between Knights

### Tower Expansion and Crafting

As your kingdom grows, you can expand your Tower by adding new Annexes. Each Annex opens new court positions and grants crafting abilities:

- **Blacksmith's Forge** — Craft weapons and equipment
- **Witch's Alchemy Room** — Brew potions and magical items

Crafted items directly assist your Knights on their quests, improving success rates and opening new narrative possibilities.

### The Time-Rewind Mechanic

Perhaps the most intriguing feature is the Demon dwelling in the Crypts of your Tower. This entity can turn back time, allowing you to retry decisions with full knowledge of what went wrong. This isn't just a convenience — it's woven into the narrative fabric of the game. You're meant to fail, learn, and rewrite fate.

This mechanic pairs well with the game's other information-gathering tools:

- **Arlin** — Your main advisor, available from day one
- **The Intendant** — Collects secrets and gossip about your Knights
- **The Lady of the Tower** — A stone statue with romance potential (yes, really)
