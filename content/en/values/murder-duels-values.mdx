## Why Murder Duels Values Matter for Every Trader

Getting ripped off in a trade stings—especially in a game with 124 unique items spanning multiple rarity tiers. Murder Duels values exist so players can reference fair prices before swapping knives, guns, finishers, or crates. Whether you just unboxed a Cosmic Karambit or you're sitting on a stack of common legendaries, knowing the current value list keeps your inventory growing instead of shrinking.

The official values are maintained by Murder Duels staff through their Discord community and published on the [Murder Duels values page](https://murderduels.com/), which also features a trade calculator. This guide breaks down every tier, highlights the most sought-after items, and gives you practical tips for evaluating trades.

## Understanding the Rarity Tiers and Value Scale

Murder Duels organizes items into several rarity categories, and each one carries a dramatically different value range. Supply—the number of copies that exist—drives pricing more than anything else. An item with only 45 copies in existence will almost always dwarf the value of one with 150,000 copies, even if both share the same rarity label.

### Rarity Breakdown

| Rarity | Value Range | Typical Supply | Examples |
|--------|------------|----------------|----------|
| Secret | 1,300 – 145,000 | 45 – 7,455 | Cosmic Sight, Seraphim |
| Collectible | 12,000 – 14,000 | ~1,000 | Chroma Sniper, Chroma Katana |
| Legendary | 100 – 80,000 | 53 – 147,119 | [OG] LMaD, Deagle |
| Epic | 10 – 40 | 23,207 – 577,214 | Ice, Hacker |

| Uncommon | 1 – 10 | 116,943 – 1,455,180 | Slap, Camo |
| Exclusive | Unvalued | 97,807 | OG |

A few important observations:

- **Secret items dominate the top.** The two most valuable items in the entire game—Cosmic Sight (145,000) and Cosmic Karambit (125,000)—are both Secret rarity with fewer than 60 copies each.
- **Legendary has the widest spread.** Values swing from 100 (Deagle, Karambit) all the way up to 80,000 ([OG] LMaD), making it the most nuanced tier for traders.
- **Epic and below are entry-level currency.** These items are useful for small adds in trades but rarely carry a deal on their own.

## Practical Trading Tips Using Murder Duels Values

Knowing the numbers is only half the battle. Here is how to apply them effectively.

### Always Check Supply Before Accepting a Trade

Two items with the same rarity tag can have wildly different values. A Deagle (Legendary, 100 value) is worth 800 times less than a [OG] LMaD (Legendary, 80,000 value). Never trade based on rarity alone—always reference the specific value.

### Use the Trade Calculator

The [Murder Duels website](https://murderduels.com/) offers a built-in trade calculator. Plug in both sides of a proposed deal and it will show whether the values align. This eliminates mental math errors and helps you spot lopsided offers instantly.

### Watch for Variant Differences

Many items have multiple variants with different copy counts and values. Space Kitty variant 1 (213 copies) is worth 31,000, while variant 2 (230 copies) is worth 28,000. That 17-copy difference matters. Always confirm which variant you are trading.

### Be Cautious with Unvalued Items

Unvalued items are risky to trade because there is no community-agreed baseline. If you own a low-supply unvalued item like Ultra Soaker (363 copies), consider holding it until an official value drops. Selling too early could mean leaving significant value on the table.

### Stack Low-Value Items for Bigger Trades

If you are working toward a high-value item, do not underestimate the power of stacking. Ten Epic items at 25 value each equals 250—enough to pair with a low-tier Legendary for something better. Small adds make deals happen.

## FAQ

### How often do Murder Duels values change?

Values are maintained by Murder Duels staff through their Discord server. There is no fixed update schedule, but high-value items with low supply tend to stay stable while mass-produced items may shift as more copies enter circulation. Check the official value list before every significant trade.

### What makes an item valuable in Murder Duels?

Supply is the primary driver. Items with fewer copies in existence—like Cosmic Sight (45 copies) or [OG] LMaD (61 copies)—command the highest Murder Duels values. Special prefixes like [OG] or [NEGATIVE] also increase demand because they signal rarity or unique editions within an already limited pool.

### Are unvalued items worth trading?

It depends on the item. Low-supply unvalued Legendaries like Ultra Soaker (363 copies) or Popsicle (461 copies) likely hold real trading potential once priced. High-supply unvalued items like Watermelon (Uncommon, 397,647 copies) are probably worth very little. If you are unsure, hold rare unvalued items until staff assign official values.

### Where can I find the most current Murder Duels values?

The most up-to-date values are available on the official [Murder Duels values page](https://murderduels.com/), which tracks all 124 items and includes a trade calculator. The staff also discusses value changes in their Discord community, so joining both resources gives you the fastest updates.
