## Why Murder Duels Skins Matter

In Roblox's Murder Duels, skins are more than cosmetic flair — they're proof of your dueling prowess. Every knife and revolver skin you unlock shows off your progression and skill level. With the game's trading system, Murder Duels skins also serve as currency between players, making them a core part of the experience.

Whether you're chasing rare revolver finishes or collecting every knife variant, understanding how the skin system works is essential. This guide covers everything from unlocking and trading to using codes that can boost your collection.

## How the Skin System Works in Murder Duels

Murder Duels gives every player two weapons per duel: a knife and a revolver. Both weapon types have their own set of skins that you can unlock and equip. The game is currently in beta, so new skins and changes to the system can arrive with any update.

### Unlocking Skins Through Progression

The primary way to earn skins is simply by playing the game. As you progress through duels and rack up wins, new knife and revolver skins become available to you. The more you play and win, the more skins you'll unlock.

Here's a quick breakdown of the progression path:

| Action | What It Contributes |
|---|---|
| Winning duels | Unlocks skins tied to match victories |
| Earning ACEs | May contribute to rarer skin unlocks |
| Playing consistently | Builds overall progression toward skin tiers |
| Trading with others | Acquires skins you haven't unlocked yet |

### Knife Skins vs. Revolver Skins

Both weapon categories have their own unique skin sets. Knife skins change the appearance of your melee weapon, while revolver skins customize your ranged option. Since you carry both into every duel, collecting skins for each weapon type lets you fully customize your loadout.

Player experience suggests that knife skins tend to be the more sought-after category, likely because knife kills feel more impactful in a game called "Murder Duels." However, revolver skins are equally important for players who rely on ranged abilities during matches.

## How to Unlock Murder Duels Skins

Unlocking skins comes down to a few key activities. Here's what you should focus on to grow your collection efficiently.

### Win Duels Consistently

Duels in Murder Duels follow a straightforward format: step on a pad to start, first to five wins takes the match. Each victory contributes to your overall progression, which in turn unlocks new skins. Focus on mastering both your knife and revolver abilities to maximize your win rate.

### Go for ACEs

An ACE is earned by eliminating every player on the opposing team in a single round by yourself during 3v3 or 4v4 matches. This is a high-skill achievement that the game specifically tracks with its own badge. Community reports indicate that ACEs may play a role in unlocking rarer or more exclusive skins, so pushing for these clutch plays can pay off beyond just bragging rights.

### Redeem Active Codes

Codes are a free way to pick up rewards in Murder Duels. The game receives new codes with updates, especially around major events like the Summer Update. To redeem codes:

1. Open Murder Duels in Roblox
2. Click the **Shop** button at the bottom of the screen
3. Select the **Codes** section
4. Type in your code and confirm

Codes can expire quickly, so it's worth checking regularly after game updates. You can find the official game page on [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/120851538706364/Murder-Duels) to stay updated on new releases and patches that might bring fresh codes.

## Trading Murder Duels Skins

One of the standout features of Murder Duels is the ability to trade skins with other players. This system adds a whole new dimension to skin collecting — you don't have to unlock everything yourself.

### How Trading Works

The game allows direct skin trades between players. While the exact trading interface may evolve as the game is still in beta, the core concept remains: you can exchange skins you own for skins another player has.

### Trading Tips

- **Know the value**: Rarer skins typically command higher trade value. Skins tied to ACEs or limited-time events are likely worth more.
- **Don't rush trades**: Take your time to evaluate offers. A skin that seems common now might become valuable if it's tied to a limited event.
- **Build duplicates**: Having extra skins gives you more bargaining power in trades.
- **Watch the meta**: If a particular skin becomes popular in the community, its trade value may increase temporarily.

### Trade Safety Checklist

| Precaution | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
