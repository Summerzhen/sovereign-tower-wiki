## Why Skins Matter in Murder Duels

If you are hunting for Murder Duels all skins, you have come to the right place. Weapon skins let you personalize your loadout and stand out in every match. Whether you prefer a flashy revolver or a sleek knife, the game offers dozens of cosmetic options across two weapon categories. This guide breaks down every confirmed skin, explains how the unlocking system works, and helps you prioritize your coin spending.

## How to Unlock Weapon Skins

Skins in Murder Duels are purely cosmetic — they change the look of your weapon but do not affect gameplay stats. According to the [MURDER Wiki on Fandom](https://roblox-murder.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_Skins), there are currently multiple variations for both revolvers and knives, and they can be obtained through two primary methods:

- **Cases:** Purchase cases for 750–825 coins. Cases grant a random skin from the available pool, so you may need to open several before landing the one you want.
- **Gamepasses:** Some skins are tied to gamepasses, which are purchased with Robux. These typically grant exclusive or premium cosmetics that cannot be earned through cases.

### Redeeming Codes for Bonus Rewards

Players can also redeem promotional codes for additional rewards that may include coins or cosmetics. To redeem codes in Murder Duels:

1. Open the game and locate the **Shop** button at the bottom of the screen.
2. Click on **Codes** inside the shop menu.
3. Type or paste your code into the text field.
4. Confirm to claim your reward.

Community reports suggest that new codes drop periodically alongside game updates, so checking back regularly is a smart habit.

## Skin Collection Strategy and Tips

Chasing every skin can get expensive if you open cases blindly. Here are practical tips to maximize your coin efficiency:

### Prioritize Your Favorite Weapon Type

Since revolver and knife skins are separate, focus on the weapon you use most. If you primarily play as the sheriff, invest in revolver cases first. If you prefer the murderer role, prioritize knife cases.

### Save Coins for Bulk Case Openings

Cases cost between 750 and 825 coins. Instead of opening one case at a time, save up for multiple openings. This approach reduces the temptation to spend coins on other shop items and keeps you focused on your skin goals.

### Track What You Already Own

Keep a mental or written list of skins you have already unlocked. Duplicate drops from cases are possible, so knowing your existing collection prevents frustration and helps you decide when to switch to the other weapon type.

### Watch for Code Drops

Promotional codes can provide free coins or even exclusive cosmetics. Content creators often share working codes during updates. Community reports indicate that codes are redeemed through the Shop > Codes menu, and new ones appear throughout the year.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many total skins are in Murder Duels?

Based on community documentation, there are over 50 revolver skins and a comparable number of knife skins. The exact count may change as developers add new cosmetics with updates. If you are trying to collect Murder Duels all skins, expect a significant time and coin investment.

### Can I trade or sell duplicate skins?

Currently, there is no official trading or marketplace system for skins in Murder Duels. Duplicate skins from case openings remain in your inventory. This makes each case opening a commitment, so spend your coins wisely.

### Do skins give any gameplay advantage?

No. All weapon skins in Murder Duels are purely cosmetic. They change the appearance of your revolver or knife but do not affect damage, speed, or any other gameplay mechanic. Your skill and strategy matter far more than your loadout appearance.

### What is the rarest skin in the game?

Community consensus points to the VIP skin and premium tier skins like Golden Eye and Drip Drop as the rarest, since they require gamepass purchases rather than random case drops. Among case-obtainable skins, the Gold and Golden collections appear to be the most coveted due to their visual prestige.
