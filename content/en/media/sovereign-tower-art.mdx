## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I find official Sovereign Tower art?

Official Sovereign Tower art is typically released through developer social media accounts, the game's official website, and patch note updates. ### Can Sovereign Tower art actually help me play better?

Yes, according to community reports and player experience. ### How do I tell the difference between fan art and official art?

Look for artist signatures, watermarks, or profile disclaimers that indicate fan creation. Official art is usually published through official developer channels with consistent branding and style. If an image appears only on a personal social media account without developer attribution, it is likely fan-created. When uncertain, treat the piece as community-sourced until you can Check it against official sources.

### Is it okay to use Sovereign Tower fan art in my own guides or posts?

You should always ask the original artist for permission before using their work, even in non-commercial guides or community posts. Most artists appreciate being asked and may grant permission with a simple credit requirement. Failing to credit or ask permission can harm community trust and discourage artists from sharing their Sovereign Tower art in the future.
