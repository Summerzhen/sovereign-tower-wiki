## Sovereign Tower at a Glance

Developed by WILD WITS, Sovereign Tower is a story-rich visual novel blended with Round Table management RPG mechanics. You play as the Sovereign of a magical tower, recruiting eccentric Knights, assigning them quests, and balancing their oversized egos while shaping your kingdom's destiny. The standout hook? If things go sideways, you can turn back time, uncover hidden secrets, and rewrite fate as many times as you need.

The game is currently available on PC, and its unique mix of narrative depth and management gameplay has caught the attention of critics right out of the gate.
