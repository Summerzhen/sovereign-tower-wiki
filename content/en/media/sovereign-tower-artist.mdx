## How the Art Serves Sovereign Tower's Gameplay

Sovereign Tower is described as a narrative roundtable management game. This hybrid genre creates specific demands for the art team. Roundtable management implies players will be making decisions about characters seated around or involved with some form of council or gathering. The art needs to support quick recognition of who's who, what they want, and how they relate to each other.

### Art Priorities for Roundtable Management Games

- **Character legibility:** Each character must be instantly recognizable from multiple angles and distances
- **Expression range:** Characters need to convey emotions that align with narrative choices
- **Environmental storytelling:** The tower and roundtable settings should reflect the game's themes
- **UI integration:** Art must work within interface layouts without cluttering strategic information

When the same artist directs both the creative vision and the visual execution, these priorities can be balanced holistically rather than negotiated between separate teams.

| Gameplay Element | Art Requirement | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Character selection | Distinctive silhouettes and color coding | Players make quick decisions about who to engage |
| Narrative scenes | Expressive poses and facial cues | Story beats land with emotional impact |
| Management interface | Clean, readable character portraits | Strategic choices aren't obscured by visual noise |
| World atmosphere | Cohesive environmental design | The tower feels like a real, lived-in place |
