## Who Is Gwendan in Sovereign Tower?

Gwendan, formally known as Sir Gwendan of Villidor, is one of the recruitable knights you can add to your Round Table in Sovereign Tower. He strides into your court declaring himself the greatest knight in all the land—and he has the charisma stats to almost back it up. For players searching for Sovereign Tower Gwendan strategies, understanding this flashy noble's strengths and glaring weaknesses is essential to using him effectively.

Gwendan brings high charisma and competitive energy to your roster, but his arrogance creates real problems on certain quest types. This guide breaks down everything you need to know about recruiting, equipping, and deploying him successfully.

## Recruiting Gwendan: What to Expect

Gwendan arrives at your tower with considerable fanfare and an ego to match. When he first appears during an audience, he introduces himself as the best knight in all the land and makes no secret of his disdain for the tower's humble condition. He refers to it as a "smelly shack" but agrees to join anyway, confident he'll elevate your realm to glory.

### Recruitment Dialogue Choices

When Gwendan arrives, you have dialogue options that affect his initial affinity. Players can choose to be welcoming or give him a bit of sass. Choosing the sassier option—telling him you might need to clean up the tower before admitting him—causes him to leave temporarily. He returns later, but that initial rejection does affect his starting relationship with you.

If you need knights urgently because you're swimming in quests, it's generally better to swallow your pride and welcome him immediately. You can always investigate his secrets and learn his preferences later through the Intendency.

## Gwendan's Stats and Preferences

Understanding Gwendan's stat profile is the key to using him well. Based on player experience from the demo, here's how his attributes break down relative to other early-game knights.

| Stat | Rating | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Charisma | High | His strongest attribute by far |
| Agility | Decent | Serviceable for the right quests |
| Strength | Moderate | Not his primary role |
| Wit | Low | Struggles on investigation quests |
| Magic | Low | Needs equipment boosts |
| Luck | Low | Unreliable for risky missions |

### Known Preferences

Gwendan's preferences shape quest outcomes significantly. Sending a knight on a quest they enjoy increases the chance of critical success and raises their affinity. Sending them on something they hate does the opposite.

- **Likes:** Competition, duels, anything that lets him show off
- **Dislikes:** Relic recovery, tasks he finds beneath his station

His competitive nature makes him a natural fit for duel-type quests and any mission where he can prove himself against an opponent. Relic recovery, on the other hand, bores him—and bored Gwendan underperforms.

### Hidden Secrets

Through the Intendency room, you can assign servants to investigate your knights and uncover gossip. When players investigated Gwendan, one of the first secrets revealed was surprising: **he's scared of the dark**. This endearing flaw beneath the bluster is useful knowledge. It suggests that quests involving dark forests, nighttime investigations, or creepy environments may not be his best fit, regardless of his stat line.

## Best Quest Assignments for Gwendan

Matching Gwendan to the right quests is where strategy meets personality management. His high charisma tempts you into sending him on diplomacy missions, but there's a catch.

### The Diplomacy Trap

Gwendan's arrogance actively works against him on diplomacy quests. Player reports from the demo show that even with high charisma, his personality caused quest failures on negotiation-type missions. The game's recap explicitly noted that "because he was arrogant, it did not work." This is a crucial lesson: stats aren't everything. A knight's disposition can override their raw numbers.

### Recommended Quest Types

| Quest Type | Effectiveness | Reason |
|------------|---------------|--------|
| Duels | Excellent | Matches his competitive nature |
| Hunts (competitive) | Good | Appeals to his desire for glory |
| Negotiation/Diplomacy | Poor | Arrogance causes failures |
| Relic Recovery | Poor | He dislikes this work |
| Investigation | Poor | Low wit and fear of dark |
| Battle/Combat | Moderate | Decent agility helps |

### Specific Quest Examples from the Demo

- **Cheese Negotiation Quest:** Sending Gwendan on this diplomacy-focused mission resulted in failure despite his charisma. Angelica, who prefers diplomacy, would have been the better pick.
- **Rabbit Hunt:** With an agility-boosting weapon like the rapier, Gwendan can handle hunt-type quests. He doesn't love hunting small game, but his stats carry him through.
- **Herdy Gertie Battle of the Bands:** This competitive music festival quest plays to Gwendan's love of spectacle and competition. He's a natural fit here.

## Equipment and Stat Optimization

Since Gwendan's wit and magic are weak, you'll need to lean on the Forge to patch his shortcomings when a quest demands those attributes.

### Weapon Recommendations

- **Rapier:** Boosts agility without sacrificing other key stats. Ideal for hunt and duel quests.
- **Wit-boosting weapons:** If you absolutely must send him on a quest requiring wit, equip a wit-enhancing weapon like the sling. Be aware this often comes with stat trade-offs.
- **Magic items:** Available at the Forge but expensive early on. Only invest if you lack other knights with magic.

### Armor Repair

Gwendan takes damage on quests like any other knight. Keep an eye on his armor condition at the Forge and repair regularly. A damaged knight sent on a dangerous quest is a recipe for failure—or worse, a major affinity loss that makes him harder to work with.

## Managing Gwendan's Affinity

Affinity represents how much a knight likes and trusts you as the Sovereign. It rises when you send them on quests they enjoy and succeed, and drops when you assign them to things they hate or when they fail.

### Tips for Keeping Gwendan Happy

- **Feed his ego.** Choose dialogue options that acknowledge his brilliance, even if it sticks in your craw.
- **Assign him to competitive quests.** Duels and contests are his sweet spot.
- **Avoid relic recovery missions.** He finds them tedious and his performance reflects that.
- **Use the Demon for do-overs.** If you accidentally send him on a terrible fit, the Demon's time-reversal power lets you redo the assignment. This is a limited resource, so save it for real mistakes.
- **Listen to his suggestions.** When he proposes tournaments and entertainment at court, acknowledging his ideas builds rapport.

### What Happens When Affinity Drops

Low affinity doesn't just mean a grumpy knight. It can lead to unexpected negative quest outcomes, refusal to cooperate, and even knights leaving your service. Gwendan's already inflated sense of self-importance means he's quicker to take offense than some other characters.

## Gwendan's Family Connection: Baroness Gwendalyn

Gwendan isn't operating in a vacuum. His aunt, Baroness Gwendalyn of Villargent, appears at court and makes her opinion of him clear. She calls him "Gwendu" and "little duckling," which suggests she sees through his bluster to the insecure young man underneath.

When Gwendalyn visits, she offers quests related to her lands and complains about the state of her garden. Accepting her requests can indirectly improve Gwendan's morale, as it shows respect for his family. Refusing her or dismissing Gwendan in front of her could create double the trouble.

## Advanced Strategies

### The Investigation Loop

Use the Intendency to investigate Gwendan repeatedly. Each round of gossip reveals new preferences and secrets that help you make better assignment decisions. Knowing he's afraid of the dark, for instance, prevents you from wasting a cycle on a doomed nighttime investigation quest.

### Time Travel Optimization

The Demon's time-reversal mechanic is especially valuable with Gwendan because his quest outcomes are less predictable. His arrogance can turn a statistically likely success into a failure. If you send him on a mission and get a bad result, rewind and try a different knight or a different equipment loadout.

### Dialogue Choices That Matter

Gwendan responds to respect and recognition. When he suggests improvements—jesters, musicians, tournaments—choosing "I shall think on it" rather than dismissing him outright keeps his affinity stable. You don't have to implement everything he wants, but acknowledging his input matters to him.

## FAQ

### Is Gwendan worth recruiting in Sovereign Tower?

Yes, despite his personality flaws. Gwendan's high charisma and competitive drive make him effective on the right quest types, particularly duels and competitive events. Just keep him away from diplomacy and relic recovery. The Sovereign Tower Gwendan dynamic is all about putting his ego where it produces results rather than problems.

### What quests should I never send Gwendan on?

Avoid diplomacy and negotiation quests—his arrogance causes failures regardless of his charisma stat. Also steer clear of relic recovery missions, dark or creepy investigations, and anything requiring high wit or magic without significant equipment investment.

### How do I find out Gwendan's hidden preferences?

Use the Intendency room to assign servants to investigate him. Each investigation cycle reveals new gossip and secrets, such as his fear of the dark. This information is critical for making smart quest assignments and avoiding costly mistakes.

### Can Gwendan's affinity be recovered if it drops?

Yes, but it takes effort. Send him on quests he enjoys (duels, competitive events), choose dialogue options that respect his opinions, and avoid repeating the mistakes that caused the drop. The Demon's time-reversal ability can also undo a bad decision before the affinity loss becomes permanent.
