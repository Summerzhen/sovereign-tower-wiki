## Who Is Lady Angelica of Clovermont?

Lady Angelica of Clovermont is the very first knight you recruit in Sovereign Tower, introduced early in the game by your advisor Frank as his dear niece. She arrives fashionably late, apologizing for getting lost on her way to the round table, and immediately endears herself by requesting a cat to brighten her cold, scary quarters. For players searching for Sovereign Tower Angelica details, she's a kindhearted, agile knight whose preferences and stats make her a versatile early-game companion—if you understand how to use her correctly.

Angelica matters because she's your starting foundation. How you build her, equip her, and assign her to quests directly shapes your early success or frustration. Misassigning her can lower her affinity and waste precious cycles.

### The Goose Quest Example

One of the clearest demonstrations of Angelica's value comes from the war goose quest. A foul-smelling man reports that a "general goose" has taken over the public baths, blocking entry for 15 days. If you send Angelica, player experience indicates she makes friends with the goose through diplomacy. The goose stays, but everyone else gets to bathe—only the smelly man remains excluded. It's a humorous, effective resolution that plays directly to her strengths.

Sending a strength-focused knight like Gberto instead results in a standard success, but he dislikes the mission. Angelica's diplomacy approach yields a more interesting and thematically appropriate outcome.

## Leveling and Equipment Strategy

Angelica starts at level one and gains experience from completed quests. When she levels up, you choose which stat to increase. Equipment from the forge can further patch her weaknesses.

### Level-Up Priority

Based on her stat distribution and quest preferences, consider this leveling order:

1. **Charisma** – Reinforces her diplomacy preference and opens more negotiation quests
2. **Agility** – Doubles down on her strongest stat for physical challenges
3. **Luck** – Her already high luck can trigger better outcomes and unexpected positive results
4. **Wit** – Only if you need her for investigation quests; otherwise, other knights handle these better

In the demo playthrough, players chose to boost her charisma first, raising it to three. This made her more effective for cheese-related diplomacy quests and similar negotiations.

### Equipment Recommendations

The forge offers weapons and gear that can temporarily boost Angelica's weaker stats. Key items include:

- **Wit Sling** – Adds +2 wit, critical for investigation quests when no other knight is available. However, it may reduce another stat, so weigh the trade-off carefully.
- **Shields** – Can boost survivability on dangerous quests without sacrificing core stats.
- **Rusty Sword/Rapier** – Agility-boosting options if you want to push her physical edge further.

Remember that equipment can be reassigned between knights at any time. You aren't locked into a purchase. If Angelica doesn't need the wit sling for a particular cycle, hand it to another knight who does.

## Angelica's Cat and Tower Interactions

Angelica's first request as a knight is deceptively important. She asks for a cat to make her room less cold and frightening. Granting this request costs you nothing and yields ongoing benefits.

After receiving the cat, Angelica names it after the Sovereign. The cat later appears in the kitchen, wandering among the spice shelves. A kitchen worker tries to coax it down, and you can interact with the cat during your daily cycles. This small detail reflects the game's attention to character relationships and makes Angelica feel more grounded in the tower.

From a gameplay perspective, fulfilling Angelica's request early builds affinity. Ignoring it or dismissing her could start your relationship on the wrong foot, making her less cooperative on future quests.

## Managing Angelica's Affinity

Affinity determines how willingly a knight serves you and can affect quest outcomes. Performing critical or great successes raises affinity, while major failures lower it. Sending a knight on quests they dislike also reduces affinity, even if they succeed.

### Affinity Tips for Angelica

- **Match quests to her preferences** whenever possible—diplomacy and animal quests are safest.
- **Avoid sending her on duels**, even if her stats could technically handle them. The affinity loss isn't worth it.
- **Use the demon's time-reversal power** if you accidentally assign her poorly. The demon allows you to undo mistakes and reassign knights without permanent consequences.
- **Check the quest requirement icons** before assigning. Red highlights indicate stat decreases; green indicates increases. If a quest requires high wit and magic, Angelica is the wrong choice without heavy equipment support.

### What Happens When Affinity Drops

Low affinity doesn't remove Angelica from your roster, but it makes her less effective. Knights with low affinity may perform worse on quests, take more damage, or produce unexpected negative outcomes. Keeping Angelica happy by respecting her preferences ensures consistent results.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What quests should I never send Angelica on in Sovereign Tower?

Avoid sending Angelica on duels, magic-based investigations, or any quest requiring high wit without equipment support. She dislikes duels due to her kindhearted nature, and her lack of magic and low wit make those quest types likely to fail or lower her affinity even on success.

### Can Angelica handle relic recovery quests?

Yes, but with caution. Relic recovery is listed as one of her preferences, but you should check the specific stat requirements of each relic quest. If the quest demands wit or magic, equip her with a wit sling or similar item first. Pure agility-based relic recovery suits her perfectly.

### How do I raise Angelica's wit stat for investigation quests?

You have two options. First, allocate wit when she levels up, though this means sacrificing charisma or agility increases. Second, purchase wit-boosting equipment like the wit sling from the forge and equip her before sending her out. The sling adds +2 wit, which can make the difference between failure and success on moderate investigation quests.

### Is the Sovereign Tower Angelica cat request important?

Yes. Granting Angelica's cat request early builds affinity and integrates her more fully into the tower's daily life. The cat appears in subsequent scenes, reinforcing that your choices have lasting narrative impact. Denying the request or ignoring it could strain your relationship with your first knight from the very beginning.
