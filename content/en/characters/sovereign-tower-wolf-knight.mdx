## How Knights Work in Sovereign Tower

Knights are the backbone of your kingdom. After you tend to your subjects each morning and accept their requests, you retreat to the Round Table to delegate those tasks to the Knights in your service. Each Knight comes with a distinct personality, moral compass (or lack thereof), and a set of five core stats that determine their effectiveness on different quest types.

### The Five Knight Stats

Every Knight in Sovereign Tower is measured across five attributes. Understanding these stats is the key to avoiding Critical Failures and chasing those sweet Critical Successes.

| Stat | What It Governs | Ideal Quest Types |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| Strength | Physical prowess and combat ability | Monster slaying, exorcisms, heavy labor |
| Agility | Speed, stealth, and reflexes | Reconnaissance, theft recovery, escape scenarios |
| Charisma | Social influence and persuasion | Diplomacy, merchant negotiations, crowd control |
| Magic | Arcane knowledge and spellcasting | Mystical investigations, curse removal, enchantments |
| Wit | Intelligence, strategy, and cunning | Scholarly research, puzzle-solving, political intrigue |

When assigning quests, matching the right stat to the right task dramatically reduces the chance of failure. As your advisor Arlin dryly notes, sending the wrong Knight—like assigning low-Charisma Ursula to a diplomatic mission—is a recipe for disaster.

### Quest Outcomes

After a Knight returns from their assigned quest, you'll receive a debriefing with one of three possible results:

- **Critical Success** — The Knight excelled, delivering bonus rewards or improved relationships.
- **Critical Failure** — The Knight botched the job, potentially damaging your standing with factions.
- **Unexpected Outcome** — Something surprising happened, opening new story branches or secrets.

These outcomes aren't purely random. A Knight's relevant stat, their satisfaction level, and the items they carry all influence the result.

## Managing Knight Satisfaction and Drama

Recruiting Knights is only half the battle. Keeping them happy is where Sovereign Tower's management layer gets genuinely stressful.

### Why Satisfaction Matters

Knights with great egos bring great drama. If a Knight's needs aren't met, they may abandon your court permanently. Sometimes that's a relief—questionable morals can make certain Knights more trouble than they're worth—but losing a high-stat Knight mid-campaign hurts.

Factors that influence Knight satisfaction include:

- **Quest assignments** — Sending a Knight on tasks suited to their stats keeps them confident and engaged.
- **Relationships with other Knights** — Drama between court members can escalate if not managed.
- **Romantic interactions** — The game allows you to explore romance with certain characters, which can strengthen bonds or create jealousy.
- **Gossip and secrets** — The Intendant collects juicy information about your Knights. Use her intel wisely to navigate interpersonal conflicts.

### The Intendant's Role

The Intendant is one of your key advisors alongside Arlin. She has a talent for uncovering secrets about your Knights, which you can leverage to make informed decisions about assignments, promotions, or even dismissals. Checking in with her regularly is a smart habit for any Sovereign trying to keep the Round Table from collapsing into chaos.

## Expanding Your Tower for Stronger Knights

Your Tower starts small, but expanding it with new Annexes directly benefits your Knights' performance on quests. Each Annex unlocks crafting capabilities that produce items to assist your court members.

### Key Annexes and Their Benefits

| Annex | Crafting Focus | Knight Benefit |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| Blacksmith's Forge | Weapons and armor | Boosts Strength and Agility on combat quests |
| Witch's Alchemy Room | Potions and elixirs | Provides Magic and healing support items |
| Additional Annexes | Various unique items | Tailored advantages for specific quest types |

Adding Annexes also opens new positions at court, allowing you to recruit more Knights. A larger Round Table means more options for quest assignment—but also more egos to balance and more drama to manage.

### Crafting Strategy

Prioritize crafting items that complement your strongest Knights' weaknesses. A wolf knight with high Strength but low Charisma, for example, might benefit from a charm potion from the Alchemy Room before being sent on a quest with any social component. Think of crafting as a way to patch gaps in your roster rather than simply doubling down on existing strengths.

## Turning Back Time: Fixing Knight Mistakes

One of Sovereign Tower's most unique mechanics is the time-loop system. Deep in the Crypts of your Tower lives a Demon who can rewind time, letting you retry decisions with full knowledge of what went wrong.

### When to Use Time Rewind

- A Knight suffered a Critical Failure on an important quest.
- You lost a valuable Knight due to satisfaction neglect.
- A faction relationship deteriorated beyond recovery.
- You discovered a secret that changes how you'd approach an earlier choice.

The time rewind mechanic makes experimentation viable. Don't be afraid to assign a Knight to a quest you're unsure about—you can always undo the outcome if it goes sideways. This is especially useful when testing whether a new recruit like a potential wolf knight performs better on certain quest types than you initially predicted.

### Secrets and the Lady of the Tower
