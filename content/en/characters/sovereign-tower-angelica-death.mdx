## How Knight Deaths Work in Sovereign Tower

Sovereign Tower uses a quest-assignment system where you send knights on missions based on their attributes and preferences. When a knight's stats fall short of what a quest demands, the mission can fail catastrophically—meaning the knight dies.

### What Causes a Quest Death

Based on player experience, knight deaths occur when key attributes are insufficient for the quest's requirements. The game evaluates whether your chosen knight meets the mission's demands, and a severe mismatch leads to fatal outcomes.

For example, in Cycle 2, **Goberto** dies attempting "The Bloody Magpie" quest because he lacks the necessary Agility. Even with equipment bonuses from the Hunting Bow, his stats don't reach the threshold needed to survive climbing the tree during the mission.

### Attribute Matching Matters More Than Preference

Each knight has preferred quest types, but preference alone won't save them. Goberto prefers Relic Recovery and Hunting—both relevant to "The Bloody Magpie"—yet he still dies because his Agility is too low. Always check the **required attributes** first, then consider preferences as a secondary factor.

| Factor | Priority | Description |

If you assign Angelica to a quest requiring high Strength or Agility—stats she doesn't excel in—you risk a fatal outcome. ## The Demon's Rewind: Your Safety Net Against Knight Deaths

Cycle 2 introduces the Crypts, where you meet the Demon. This character offers a powerful mechanic: the ability to **rewind time** to any earlier cycle in the current act. This is essentially an in-game save rewind that preserves your progress in key areas.

### What the Demon's Rewind Preserves

When you use the Demon's power to turn back time, you keep:

- **All acquired knowledge** from previous cycles
- **Sovereign Archetype progress** (e.g., Wisdom levels earned)
- **New dialogue options** unlocked by your prior experiences

This means rewinding isn't starting over—it's starting over smarter. You know which quests fail, which knights die, and how to reassign missions for better outcomes.

### What Changes After Rewinding

The audience sequence may differ after a rewind. Characters who appeared before might not show up, or new faces could present different opportunities. This variability means you can't simply copy your previous choices—you still need to think critically about each assignment.

## Forge Preparation: Equipment That Saves Lives

The Forge unlocks during Cycle 2 after visiting the Gallery and speaking with the Lady of the Tower. It provides two critical functions:

### Armor Repair

Knights need repaired armor before heading out on quests. Sending a knight with damaged armor increases the risk of failure or death. Always check armor status and repair as needed before finalizing quest assignments.

### Weapon Shop

The Forge's shop offers weapons that boost specific attributes. For example, the Hunting Bow grants +1 Wit and +1 Agility—useful for shoring up a knight's weak spots. However, some weapons are locked behind requirements, so plan your purchases carefully.

| Equipment | Bonus | Best For |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| Hunting Bow | +1 Wit, +1 Agility | Knights needing Agility for quests like The Bloody Magpie |
| Armor Repair | Restores defense | Any knight before quest assignment |

Equipment can bridge small stat gaps, but it won't compensate for a fundamental mismatch. If a knight is several points short of a quest's requirements, no single item will save them. In those cases, reassign the quest to a better-suited knight.
