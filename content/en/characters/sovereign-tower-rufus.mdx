## Rufus of Groveshire: Lore and Background

Rufus introduces himself as the last of his family line, and potentially the last of his kind entirely. That weight of extinction follows him through every word he speaks to the Sovereign. He's not a character who stumbled into the Tower by accident; he heard whispers through the forest about your arrival and came willingly, drawn to the life returning to those cold stone halls.

### A Life Beneath Open Skies

Before coming to the Tower, Rufus lived entirely in the wild. He spent years beneath canopies of oak and ash, with the moon as his only ceiling and the dirt as his floor. His dialogue makes clear that the wilderness isn't just where he lived—it's who he is. He describes loving time deep in the woods, frolicking in nature, sniffing for prey, and marking territory. There's a feral quality to his identity that no amount of fine clothing can fully suppress.

Key aspects of Rufus's background include:

- **Last of his line**: He carries the burden of being the final member of his family
- **Possibly the last of his kind**: Hints at a deeper, more tragic extinction
- **Wild upbringing**: Years of untethered existence in forests
- **Drawn to the Tower**: Chose to come rather than being forced or summoned
- **Howling into the dark**: His solitude was marked by unanswered calls

The imagery of howling into silence and belonging to nothing paints Rufus as a character defined by isolation—until he found the Sovereign.

## Rufus's Personality and Loyalty Traits

Rufus is straightforward about who he is and what he offers. He doesn't sugarcoat his nature, and that honesty is central to his appeal. Here's a breakdown of what defines him as a character.

Rufus admits he doesn't take well to strangers. He's not the type to warm up instantly to everyone he meets. But for those he claims as his own—those he chooses to run beside—his devotion is absolute. He states he would sooner die than see harm come to them.

This selective loyalty makes him a powerful narrative companion. He's not a generic ally; he's someone who must be earned, and once that bond forms, it runs deeper than duty.

One of the most compelling things about Rufus is his self-awareness. This tension between civilization and instinct gives him dimension beyond a simple loyal warrior archetype.
