## How the Knight Recruitment System Works

Before you can worry about any specific Knight, you need to understand how recruitment functions in Sovereign Tower. The game doesn't just hand you a full roster on day one. You earn your court over time through choices, story progression, and how you expand your Tower.

### The Basics of Building Your Round Table

Your Round Table is the heart of your kingdom's operations. Here's how the system flows:

1. **Tend to your subjects each morning** – Listen to requests ranging from mundane gardening tasks to full-blown exorcisms.
2. **Accept obligations** – The problems you agree to address become quests that need Knight assignments.
3. **Assign the right Knight** – Each quest requires different skills, so matching the right hero to the job reduces the chance of failure.
4. **Review the debrief** – Knights return with results that fall into Critical Success, Critical Failure, or Unexpected Outcome categories.

The more you expand your Tower with new Annexes, the more positions open up at court. This means more Knights can join your roster—but also more egos to balance.

### Knight Stats Explained

Every Knight in Sovereign Tower has five core stats that determine their effectiveness on quests:

| Stat | What It Affects | Best For |
|------|----------------|----------|
| Strength | Physical challenges, combat quests | Monster slaying, guard duties |
| Agility | Speed-based tasks, stealth missions | Reconnaissance, theft recovery |
| Charisma | Social interactions, persuasion | Diplomacy, negotiations |
| Magic | Supernatural encounters, enchantments | Exorcisms, mystical problems |
| Wit | Puzzle-solving, strategy quests | Investigations, scholarly tasks |

The game's advisor, Arlin, even jokes about this system—mentioning that you probably shouldn't send Ursula on a quest requiring Charisma. This kind of personality-driven stat assignment is key to understanding each Knight's role, including where characters like Sparky might fit.
