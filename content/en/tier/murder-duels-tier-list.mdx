## How to Evaluate Abilities for Your Own Tier List

Since community consensus is still forming, the most practical approach is learning how to rank abilities yourself. Below is a structured framework you can use to evaluate any ability in Murder Duels.

### Core Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | What to Ask Yourself | Weight |
|-----------|---------------------|--------|
| Damage Output | Does this ability consistently deal high damage per use? | High |
| Cooldown Efficiency | Is the cooldown short enough to use the ability multiple times per duel? | High |
| Versatility | Does this ability work well across different matchups and situations? | Medium |
| Ease of Use | Can most players land this ability reliably, or does it require high skill? | Medium |
| Synergy Potential | Does this ability combo well with other popular picks? | Medium |

### Tier Definitions

When building your murder duels tier list, use these standard tier definitions so your rankings are easy for others to interpret:

- **S-Tier:** Meta-defining abilities. Almost always the optimal pick. Strong in nearly every matchup.
- **A-Tier:** Excellent choices that perform consistently well. Small weaknesses keep them from S-Tier.
- **B-Tier:** Solid, reliable abilities. Good for most duels but may struggle against top-tier picks.
- **C-Tier:** Niche or situational abilities. Can work in the right hands or specific matchups.
- **D-Tier:** Generally outclassed. Only worth using if no better option is available.

## Common Mistakes Players Make When Tiering Abilities

Even experienced players fall into traps when evaluating abilities. Watch out for these pitfalls.

### Recency Bias

You just won five duels in a row with a specific ability, so you immediately rank it S-Tier. But a small sample size can be misleading. Always base rankings on a meaningful number of matches.

### Confusing Fun with Power

Some abilities are genuinely enjoyable to use but are not actually competitive. It is fine to have a "fun factor" ranking, but keep it separate from your competitive tier list.

### Ignoring Matchup Context

No ability exists in a vacuum. A defensive ability might be C-Tier against aggressive players but A-Tier against opponents who rely on slow, powerful attacks. Consider matchup spreads when placing abilities.

### Overvaluing Low-Cooldown Abilities

Fast cooldowns are great, but not if the ability itself deals negligible damage or has minimal impact. An ability you can use twice as often is only better if each use contributes meaningfully to winning the duel.

### Undervaluing Niche Picks

Some abilities only shine in specific scenarios. Do not automatically dump them into D-Tier. Instead, clearly note the situations where they excel so other players can make informed choices.

## Practical Tips for Using Any Tier List Effectively

Whether you are using your own murder duels tier list or someone else's, these tips will help you get more value out of it.

### Tier Lists Are Starting Points, Not Rules

A tier list tells you what generally works well. It does not account for your personal playstyle, skill level, or the specific opponents you face. Use rankings as a guide, then adapt based on your actual results.

### Prioritize S- and A-Tier Abilities First

If you are new or working with limited resources, focus on unlocking and mastering top-tier abilities before experimenting with lower-ranked options. This gives you the strongest foundation.

### Do Not Neglect Synergy

An A-Tier ability that combos perfectly with your build may outperform an S-Tier ability that does not fit your strategy. Always consider how abilities work together.

### Re-evaluate Regularly

Set a reminder to revisit your tier list every few weeks. As the meta shifts and you improve as a player, your rankings should evolve too.

A murder duels tier list is a ranking system that categorizes abilities in the game from best to worst based on factors like damage, cooldown efficiency, versatility, and overall competitive performance. Players use these rankings to decide which abilities to prioritize.
